## How to break in: off-campus routes, Women in X programs, early insight

### Practical routes if you're not on a target campus
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=cs,math,business,econ; region=global; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=; format=in-person -->
- **Bank applications are open to off-campus students.** GS, JPM and MS India accept direct applications when their India roles are posted, and use the same OA. Apply in July–September of your 3rd year; by November most seats are gone.
- **Hackathons and challenges that lead to interviews:** JPM Code for Good (registrations ~Mar), Morgan Stanley Code to Give (Aug–Sep), Flipkart GRiD, Walmart CodeHers (women), D. E. Shaw / Tower / Quadeye campus contests, Optiver's and IMC's online trading games, WorldQuant IQC. These are the highest-yield off-campus doors in finance.
- **Women-in-X programs:** GS India's Women Emerging in Finance (leading engineering and business schools), JPM Winning Women (US, Singapore, Australia, Japan only), D. E. Shaw Ascend Educare (Jul–Aug; 2nd/3rd-year women), Jane Street INSIGHT/WiSE, Walmart CodeHers, Citi and Barclays women's insight days, McKinsey's Next Generation Women Leaders (mostly Americas/Europe; occasional India events), BCG and Bain women-in-consulting campus events. Apply in 1st/2nd year; they're pre-internship by design.
- **Global quant firms don't care about your campus:** Jane Street, HRT, Citadel, Optiver and Two Sigma all interview from the website. Your CP rating, olympiad record and probability speed are the currency.
- **Consulting off-campus** realistically means BCG X, Accenture, Deloitte, EY-Parthenon and case-competition wins, not MBB consulting interns.
- **Keep a referral list.** Bank and quant employees in India regularly refer; ask with a specific req ID and a 3-line pitch.
