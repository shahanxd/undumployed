## Big Tech India

### Amazon SDE I Intern (2027 batch)
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=cs; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** SDE Internship (6 months) | **Location:** Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Delhi NCR, Mumbai | **Funded:** ✅ Paid (~₹80k–1.1L/month reported; not published)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `Rolling`, **live now** |
| Duration | 24–26 weeks full-time, start between Jan–Jun 2027 |
| Who | Bachelor's/Master's in CS or related, **graduating 2027**. One application covers all India locations. |
| Stipend | Paid; relocation support varies |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** Amazon's main India intern pipeline is a 6-month final-semester internship, not a 10-week summer. You ship production code on a team with an SDE mentor; strong performers get a PPO. Amazon also posts **Applied Scientist Intern** (MS/PhD, ML) and shorter summer SDE roles for pre-final years around Oct–Dec.

**Reality check:** The online assessment (2 DSA problems + work-style survey) is the gate, and most rejections happen there. Your college must allow a 6-month off-campus internship in 8th sem. Apply the week a posting goes up; rolling means the pipeline fills.

**Apply:** [amazon.jobs → SDE I Intern, Amazon University Talent Acquisition](https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10508052/sde-i-intern-amazon-university-talent-acquisition)

## How Big Tech intern hiring works in India

- **On-campus is the main pipe.** Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Qualcomm, TI, Uber, Atlassian, Walmart, Salesforce and LinkedIn fill most of their India intern seats through placement cells at IITs, NITs, BITS, IIITs and a rotating list of other colleges, in **Aug–Oct** (summer) and **Nov–Jan** (6-month). If your college is on the list, the placement cell is your main route, and you usually get one attempt per company.
- **Off-campus is possible but timing-critical.** Amazon (6-month SDE), Google SWE/STEP/Student Researcher, Microsoft (some reqs), Adobe, NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Uber, Razorpay, CRED and Sarvam accept direct applications. Reqs are open 1–4 weeks; apply within 48 hours of posting. Set alerts on the **official** career sites, not on aggregators.
- **Referrals move your resume to the top of a screen, not past interviews.** They help most at Google, Microsoft, Amazon and Uber. Ask alumni or people you've met at hackathons, with a 3-line message and a link to something you've built.
- **Contests are the third door.** Flipkart GRiD, Walmart CodeHers and the Cisco Ideathon convert to interviews (quant-firm contests are in [finance-quant-consulting.md](finance-quant-consulting.md)). So does GSoC for Google research/SWE screens.
- **What the screens test.** SWE: 2 DSA rounds (LeetCode medium–hard) + basic system/LLD for 3rd-years. Hardware: digital electronics, C, computer architecture. Research: papers + a research talk. PM/design: portfolio/case. CGPA gates are typically 6.0–7.0/10 (Atlassian ≥6; Uber ≥65%).
- **6-month vs summer.** Amazon, PhonePe, Uber, NVIDIA, AMD and most Indian product companies prefer 6-month final-semester interns (Jan–Jun). Summer (May–Jul) 8–12-week roles are for pre-final years and are the ones that lead to PPOs a year early.
- **Meta and Apple have no structured India intern program.** Meta's internships are in the US, EMEA and Singapore; from an Indian college, the realistic Meta route is the Bangalore university-grad role in final year, so ignore "Meta internship India" listings on aggregators. Google has no India APM (APM is full-time, US); Anthropic and OpenAI have no student internships anywhere, only the residency/fellowship routes above.
