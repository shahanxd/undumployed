## Big national programs (apply once, many institutes)

### IAS–INSA–NASI Summer Research Fellowship (SRFP)
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=cs,ai,ee,mech,civil,physics,math,chem,bio,med; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=indian-only; gates=enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Research Fellowship (placement at any of ~1,000+ approved guides across IISc, IITs, IISERs, TIFR, CSIR, universities) | **Location:** Across India | **Funded:** ✅ Monthly fellowship + round-trip train fare

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Nov 30, 2026** (SRFP 2027 is open now) |
| Duration | 2 months, typically May–July 2027 (guide can agree to other months) |
| Who | Students at UGC/AICTE/accredited Indian institutions: BSc/BS 2nd yr, BE/BTech/BCA 2nd–3rd yr, BPharm 3rd–4th, MSc/MTech/ME 1st yr, integrated MSc/BS-MS 2nd–4th yr, MBBS 2nd–4th yr, Int. PhD 1st–2nd yr. **65%+ average in core subjects from Class X onward.** PhD students not eligible. Max two awards lifetime. Also open to college/university teachers. |
| Stipend | Monthly fellowship (₹12,500/mo for students in recent cycles; confirm in the 2027 PDF) + sleeper/second-class train fare. Host institute usually gives hostel. |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** The single biggest research-internship pipeline in India, run jointly by the three national science academies. You name a specific guide (from the approved list) and write a 150–250 word proposal on what you want to do in their lab; the Academy fetches a recommendation from one of your current teachers. Results and guide concurrence come around March–April 2027. It is the standard way external students get into IISc, TIFR-H, NCBS, JNCASR, PRL and the IISERs.

**Reality check:** ~1,500–2,000 selected out of tens of thousands. The proposal must be specific to the guide's work. Generic "I love physics" write-ups are the main reason for rejection. If you are not selected, many host labs still take SRFP-style visitors on their own funds; the guide list is a good cold-email directory.

**Apply:** [webjapps.ias.ac.in/fellowship2027](https://webjapps.ias.ac.in/fellowship2027/index.html) · [Instructions](https://webjapps.ias.ac.in/fellowship2027/application_instructions.jsp)

<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=any; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=indian-only; gates=enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Research Internship (portal) | **Location:** Gandhinagar, Gujarat | **Funded:** ✅ ₹2,000/week for up to 8 weeks (₹16,000)

|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `~Mar 5, 2027` (2026: opened Feb 16, closed Mar 7 after extension) |
| Duration | Min 8 weeks between ~May 7 and Jul 16, 2027 |
| Who | Any bachelor's or master's student (Indian or international students studying in India); all disciplines incl. humanities, design, social science |

### IIT Delhi: Summer Research Fellowship Programme
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=cs,ai,ee,mech,civil,chem; region=india; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=noc,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Research Fellowship (portal) | **Location:** New Delhi | **Funded:** ✅ ₹2,000/week + free hostel boarding/lodging + sleeper-class fare

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `~Apr 3, 2027` 5 PM (2026: portal open Mar 16 to Apr 3; results first week of May) |
| Duration | 8 weeks, ~May 13 to Jul 13, 2027 |
| Who | Students at engineering institutes **other than IIT Delhi** who are within the **top 10 ranks** of their programme/branch. UG: completed 2 years. PG (MTech/ME): completed 1st semester + 70% / CGPA 7.0. Needs a rank certificate and an NOC from your institute. |
| Stipend | ₹2,000/week (raised 4× in 2026 from ₹500). You pay your own accident insurance. |

### IIT Bombay: Research Internship Award (semester-long)
<!-- tags: stage=ug; field=any; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Research Internship (portal, project-specific) | **Location:** Mumbai | **Funded:** ✅ ₹15,000/month for 4–6 months; shared campus accommodation subject to availability

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Next: `~Aug–Sep 2027` (2026–27 cycle closed **Sep 29, 2026**; shortlist Oct 13, tests/interviews Oct 26–Nov 5, results by Dec 10, joining Dec 21, 2026 to Jan 20, 2027) |
| Duration | 4–6 months, full-time, starting Dec/Jan |
| Who | 3rd/4th-year bachelor's students in the top 20% of their discipline; must work full-time on the listed project |
| Stipend | ₹15,000/month fixed |
| Stage | UG |

**What it is:** IRCC (IIT Bombay's R&D office) lists specific sponsored projects; you apply against a project, sit a test/interview, and join for a semester. It is IITB's only institute-wide stipended route for external students; it is not a summer program.

**Reality check:** You need a semester off or a final-year project arrangement with your college. For summer, IITB has **no central program**. Use the Academic Office "Projects/Summer Training for External Students" route (Indian nationals only; you find the IITB guide yourself, fees apply) or a centre-run program like Trust Lab below.

**Apply:** [ircc.iitb.ac.in → IITB Internship](https://www.ircc.iitb.ac.in/IRCC-Webpage/IITBInternship/) · Faculty-first summer route: [acad.iitb.ac.in/admissions/internship](https://acad.iitb.ac.in/admissions/internship)

**Apply:** [isro.gov.in/Internship.html](https://www.isro.gov.in/Internship.html) → centre websites (e.g. [iirs.gov.in](https://www.iirs.gov.in/), [vssc.gov.in](https://www.vssc.gov.in/), [ursc.gov.in](https://www.ursc.gov.in/))
