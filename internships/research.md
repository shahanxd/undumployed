> **Type:** Research Internship | **Location:** Mumbai | **Funded:** ✅ Varies by project

### CERN Summer Student Programme
> **Type:** Research Internship | **Location:** Geneva, Switzerland | **Funded:** ✅ CHF 2,900/month + travel

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Late January (applications open November/December) |
| Duration | 2 months (June–August) |
| Who | Min 3 years of university completed. Physics, CS, Engineering, Mathematics. |

**What it is:** Work on actual CERN experiments — particle physics, computing, engineering. One of the most prestigious research experiences available to undergrads globally.
