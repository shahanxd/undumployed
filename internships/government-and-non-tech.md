# Internships: Government, Policy, Design, Law, Media & Social Sector

> Internships in Indian government bodies, international organisations, think tanks, economics research labs, courts and law firms, newsrooms, design/creative studios and product management: the non-tech side of the internship market, with notes on what's unpaid and what needs an NOC. Tech → [tech-companies.md](tech-companies.md). Finance/consulting → [finance-quant-consulting.md](finance-quant-consulting.md). Fellowships (LAMP, Teach For India, SBI YFI, CM fellowships) → [fellowships/india.md](../fellowships/india.md).

**Jump to:** [Government](#government) · [International organisations](#international-organisations) · [Economics & policy research](#economics--policy-research) · [Think tanks & legal policy](#think-tanks--legal-policy) · [Law](#law) · [Social sector](#social-sector) · [Media & journalism](#media--journalism) · [Design, creative & product](#design-creative--product)

**NOC / bona fide rule of thumb:** every government body (NITI, MEA, RBI, SEBI, DRDO, ISRO, CCI, NHRC, courts) requires a bona fide certificate or NOC from your institution, and most ask for it at application, not joining. Get a signed template from your dean's office now.

## Government

### NITI Aayog Internship
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd; field=any; region=india; funding=unpaid; citizenship=any; gates=noc,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Policy Internship | **Location:** New Delhi (in-office) | **Funded:** ❌ Unpaid (certificate on completion)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Oct 1–10, 2026**; the portal accepts applications only from the **1st to 10th of every month**, for internships starting 2–6 months later (apply no later than 2 months before your start month) |
| Duration | 6 weeks minimum, up to 6 months |
| Who | Bona fide UG students who have completed at least 2 years with ≥85% in Class 12, PG students with ≥70% in graduation, research scholars; any discipline incl. engineering, economics, law, social sciences. NOC/bona fide letter from your institution is mandatory |
| Stipend | None |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** Attachment to a NITI vertical (health, education, energy, data & analytics, evaluation, frontier tech) producing a report or dashboard for a senior officer. The most-recognised government policy internship for undergrads.

**Reality check:** Unpaid and Delhi-only; selection is by vertical need, so name a vertical and skill (Stata/R/GIS/policy writing) in your application. Many applicants, few slots. Apply 2–3 months ahead of when you can start.

**Apply:** [workforindia.niti.gov.in](https://workforindia.niti.gov.in/) · scheme details: [niti.gov.in/internship](https://www.niti.gov.in/internship)

### RBI Summer Internship & Research Internship
<!-- tags: stage=pg,phd,grad; field=econ,business,math,law,ai; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=noc; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Summer Placement (PG and law students) / Research Internship (post-degree) | **Location:** Mumbai (central office, DEPR/DSIM), regional offices | **Funded:** ✅ Stipend

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Research Internship** (batches start Jan 1 and Jul 1): applications **Jul–Nov 2026** for the Jan 2027 batch, then Jan–May 2027 for the Jul 2027 batch; email the form, CV, references and SoP to the department you want. **Summer Internship** (Apr–Jul 2027): applications `~Oct–Dec 2026` via the RBI Opportunities portal. ⚠️ Unverified: the summer call has run mid-Oct to mid-Dec in recent years; confirm on the portal |
| Duration | Summer: 2–3 months. Research: 6 months, extendable six months at a time (2 years max) |
| Who | Summer: penultimate-year students of a PG course (management, statistics, law, commerce, economics, econometrics, banking, finance), a 5-year integrated law course or a 3-year LLB; up to 125 interns. Research: Indian or foreign graduates heading for a PhD, with a PG in economics, statistics, finance or international trade, an MBA (Finance), or a B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc (CS) or data-science degree for DSIM/DEPR |
| Stipend | Research ₹45,000/month (find your own housing in Mumbai). Summer ~₹20,000/month ⚠️ Unverified |
| Stage | UG (law) / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** The Summer Internship attaches you to a department (monetary policy, financial stability, fintech, legal, HR) for a supervised project. The Research Internship (DEPR, DSIM, Financial Stability or International Department; at most 20 a year) is a real pre-doc: you co-author working papers with RBI economists, and it's a recognised springboard to econ PhDs abroad.

**Reality check:** Summer slots are few (up to 125) and skew to top B-schools, DSE, JNU, ISI, IGIDR, NLUs. Research Internship wants econometrics + coding (Stata/R/Python) and a writing sample. Law is the one undergraduate route: penultimate-year LLB and 5-year integrated law students can apply.

**Apply:** [opportunities.rbi.org.in](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in/) → Internships · Research Internship scheme and form: [rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/bs_viewcontent.aspx?Id=3496)

### MEA Internship Programme
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,grad; field=any; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=indian-only; gates=noc,age-limit; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Foreign-policy internship (Ministry of External Affairs) | **Location:** New Delhi (South Block / Jawaharlal Nehru Bhawan) | **Funded:** ✅ ₹10,000/month + one return economy airfare from your state capital

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Two terms a year. **Term II 2026-27** (Oct–Mar): **Oct 5, 2026**, 4 pm (extended from Sep 27). **Term I** (Apr–Sep 2027): next call `~Jan–Feb 2027` (2026 call: Feb 10–Mar 4). Each term takes applicants only from its own states/UTs. Term II: Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Punjab, Uttarakhand, West Bengal, Odisha, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, J&K, Ladakh, Puducherry, Lakshadweep; all other states/UTs apply in Term I. Notices and timelines are under the portal's "Internship" tab |
| Duration | 1–3 months within the term |
| Who | Indian citizens with a graduate degree when applying (final-year UG only if an internship is a compulsory part of the course); age ≤ 25 on Dec 31 of the internship year; state-wise merit lists on Class 12 and graduation marks, priority to aspirational (TADP) districts at screening and to SC/ST/OBC/EWS at interview, at least 30% women; up to 30 interns per term. NOC/bona fide required |
| Stipend | ₹10,000/month; travel reimbursed; accommodation is your own |
| Stage | UG (final year) / PG |

**What it is:** Placement in a territorial or functional division of MEA, producing briefs and research for diplomats. It's the closest thing to an IFS taster. A separate **MEA Law Internship** track (Legal & Treaties Division) takes law students.

**Reality check:** Competitive, but state-wise merit lists and priority for aspirational districts mean applicants from small-town India have a real shot. Apply as early in the window as possible; a clear regional/thematic interest statement matters.

**Apply:** [internship.mea.gov.in](https://internship.mea.gov.in/)

### CCI, NHRC, NALSA, CAG, ECI internships
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd; field=law,econ,policy,business,media; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=noc,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Regulatory / rights / audit / election-administration internships | **Location:** New Delhi (all HQs); CAG also Mumbai, Bhopal, Chennai, Bhubaneswar, Guwahati, Rajkot and its field audit offices; ECI also some state offices; NHRC also runs online batches | **Funded:** ✅ Honorarium at CCI (₹15,000/month) and NHRC (₹12,000 for the 4-week programme); ❌ CAG, NALSA and ECI unpaid

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **CCI**: `Rolling`, one-month slots from the 1st; apply online by the 1st of the previous month (e.g. by Nov 1 for December). **NHRC** (2026–27 calendar): winter in-person internship **Dec 14, 2026–Jan 8, 2027**, call `~late Oct–Nov 2026` (2025 call: Oct 21–Nov 10); summer in-person internship in June, call `~Apr 2027` (2026: Apr 13–27); 2-week online batches (OSTI) Nov 16–27, 2026, Feb 1–12 and Mar 8–19, 2027, each advertised about a month ahead. **NALSA**: `Rolling`, one-month slots picked by draw of lots; each month's form is open from four months to one month ahead (January slots: Sep 1–Nov 30). **CAG**: `Rolling`; apply between the 1st and 20th of any month. **ECI**: ⚠️ Unverified, last known `Rolling`; watch eci.gov.in |
| Duration | CCI 1 month (2–3 months with your institution's recommendation); NHRC 4 weeks in-person or 2 weeks online; NALSA 1 month; CAG 4 weeks–6 months; ECI 1–3 months |
| Who | CCI: Indian nationals in MA/MSc economics, MBA, LLM, LLB (2nd yr+ of 3-yr; 4th yr+ of 5-yr), the final years of a 5-yr integrated economics course, or CA/CS/CMA final; not open if you already hold a job offer. NHRC in-person: LLB (same years as CCI), LLM or social-science PG, ≥60% marks, age ≤ 28; NHRC online: 3rd/final-year UG, PG, PhD. NALSA: law students (priority to 3rd yr+ of 5-yr and 2nd yr+ of 3-yr). CAG: UG after 2nd year with ≥80% in Class 12; PG after 1st year or PhD with ≥60% in graduation. ECI: PG students/research scholars in social sciences, law, management, mass comm. NOC/bona fide required everywhere |
| Stipend | CCI ₹15,000/month; NHRC ₹12,000 for the 4-week in-person programme plus 3AC train fare, ₹2,000 for an online batch; NALSA and CAG unpaid; ECI none |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** Regulator and constitutional-body internships that carry real weight for law, economics and public-policy CVs. CCI (competition law and economics) is the most substantive; NHRC's summer/winter programs include field visits to prisons and shelters; ECI's is popular before election years.

**Reality check:** Mostly Delhi, short, and paperwork-heavy. CCI takes up to 15 interns a month; NHRC publishes a yearly calendar but each call stays open only 2–3 weeks; NALSA picks by draw of lots; CAG lets you apply twice a financial year.

**Apply:** [cci.gov.in/internship](https://www.cci.gov.in/internship) · [nhrc.nic.in → Internship programmes](https://nhrc.nic.in/training-programmes/internship-programmes) · [nalsa.gov.in → Internship Programme](https://nalsa.gov.in/internship-programme-2026/) · [cag.gov.in → Student Internship Programme](https://cag.gov.in/en/student-internship-programme) · [eci.gov.in](https://www.eci.gov.in/)

### SEBI, Ministry of Finance (DEA), MeitY, NIC internships
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=law,business,econ,policy,cs,ai; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=noc,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Regulator / ministry internships | **Location:** New Delhi (MeitY, NIC, DEA); Mumbai (SEBI) | **Funded:** ✅ ₹10,000/month at MeitY/NIC; DEA paid ₹10,000 (PG) / ₹20,000 (PhD) when it last ran

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **MeitY Digital India Internship Scheme** (also places interns at NIC and STQC): next call `~Apr 2027` for Jun–Jul internships (2026 applications ran Apr 10–29; 2025: Apr 4–24). **SEBI**: no student internship call since its IT-department internship in 2022; it now hires graduates through the post-degree Young Professional Program (last call Feb 10, 2026). **DEA**: last Internship Scheme call was Jun 2, 2022 (for 2022-23), none since; watch dea.gov.in → Careers |
| Duration | MeitY/NIC: 2 months (Jun 1–Jul 31 in 2026), extendable to 3; DEA (2022-23): 3–6 months |
| Who | MeitY/NIC: Indian students at Indian universities with ≥60% in their last exam, in the second-last year of their course (final-year students are excluded): B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech/M.Sc in CS, IT, electronics or communications, or MCA; LL.B students for the internet and online-gaming policy slots; economics/statistics undergraduates for the digital-economy slot; your institution must forward the application. DEA (2022-23): PG/PhD students in economics, law, finance or management from NIRF top-25 or QS-ranked institutions only. Bona fide/NOC required |
| Stipend | MeitY/NIC: ₹10,000/month token stipend, paid after your report is accepted. DEA (2022-23): ₹10,000/month for PG, ₹20,000 for PhD |

**What it is:** MeitY's Digital India Internship Scheme is the live one: 51 slots in 2026 (29 in 2025) across MeitY, NIC (the government's IT backbone; real e-governance code) and STQC, in cyber security, AI, digital public infrastructure, semiconductors, internet policy, design and content. DEA (macro/fiscal policy divisions in North Block) and SEBI (market regulation, legal, IT) have run small intern schemes before, but not since 2022.

**Reality check:** MeitY's window is about three weeks in April and needs your institution to forward the application, so get the letter ready by March. Don't plan around SEBI or DEA. Along with DRDO and ISRO, NIC/MeitY are the technical options among the government internships here.

**Apply:** [intern.meity.gov.in](https://intern.meity.gov.in/) (MeitY, NIC, STQC) · [SEBI → Young Professional Program / Internship](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/about/AboutAction.do?doInternships=yes) · [dea.gov.in → Careers](https://dea.gov.in/career-vacancy)

### DRDO
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=ee,mech,cs,ai,physics,chem,bio; region=india; funding=unpaid; citizenship=indian-only; gates=noc,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Research/project internship at DRDO labs | **Location:** 50+ labs (Delhi: SSPL/INMAS/LASTEC; Bengaluru: CAIR/ADE/GTRE/LRDE; Hyderabad: RCI/DRDL; Pune: ARDE/HEMRL; Dehradun: IRDE; etc.) | **Funded:** ❌ Mostly unpaid (a few labs pay a token stipend)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|

|-------|---------|

### World Bank Group Internship
<!-- tags: stage=pg,phd; field=econ,business,policy,civil,bio,med,edu,ai,media; region=global; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Paid internship, now called **WBG Pioneers** (World Bank, IFC, MIGA) | **Location:** Washington DC + country offices incl. New Delhi/Chennai | **Funded:** ✅ Paid hourly + travel allowance for HQ

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Two cycles a year. **Spring/Summer:** applications `~Jan–Feb 2027` (interviews Mar, selection Apr). **Winter/Fall:** applications Jul–Aug, interviews Sep, selection Oct. Open positions are listed on the program page; apply to at most three |
| Duration | 4 weeks minimum (usually 8–12) |
| Who | **Final-year undergraduates**, or students currently in a **Master's or PhD**; citizen of a WBG member country (India qualifies); 0–6 years' work experience; fluent English. Fields include economics, investment, health/education, social sciences, agriculture, environment, engineering, urban planning and corporate functions (finance, IT, HR, communications) |
| Stipend | Hourly pay; HQ interns get a travel allowance |
