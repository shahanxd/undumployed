### Jane Street SEE India
> **Type:** Intensive program | **Location:** India (virtual + in-person sessions) | **Funded:** ✅ Fully covered

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **September 27, 2026** |
| Program dates | SE track: Dec 8–14, 2026 · T&R track: Dec 11–17, 2026 |
| Who | Students graduating 2029–2030 |
| Skills | Strong mathematics, probability, logical reasoning |

**What it is:** An intensive week-long program — not a traditional multi-month internship. But it's the most elite pathway to Jane Street full-time and internship offers available in India. Extremely selective.

**Reality check:** Jane Street is looking for mathematical sharpness above all else. LeetCode grinding is less relevant here than probability puzzles, mental math, and logical thinking. Practice with Jane Street's own puzzle collections.

**Apply:** [janestreet.com/programs-and-events](https://www.janestreet.com/programs-and-events/)

### BARC Training School
> **Type:** Career program (OCES/DGFS) | **Location:** Mumbai | **Funded:** ✅ ₹56,100/month → Scientific Officer on absorption

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | February–May annually |
| Duration | 1 year training → permanent absorption as Scientific Officer |
| Who | Engineering graduates (all branches: ME, EE, CS, CE, Chemical, Metallurgy, Instrumentation) via GATE score OR BARC exam |

**What it is:** Not a short internship. 1-year training at Bhabha Atomic Research Centre leading to a permanent government career as Scientific Officer. Nuclear, defense, materials, reactor engineering.

**Reality check:** Career-grade commitment — you're signing up for a government science career, not a summer experience.

**Apply:** [barc.gov.in](https://www.barc.gov.in) → Training School
