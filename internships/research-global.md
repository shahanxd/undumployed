## Read this first

- **Portal vs faculty-first.** Portal programs (CERN, ETH SSRF, Summer@EPFL, OIST, Aalto, TIIP) have a form and a date. Faculty-first programs (Max Planck, KAIST, HKUST, DAAD WISE, Charpak) give you nothing until a professor says yes. Start cold-emailing 2–3 months before the scholarship deadline, with a 1-paragraph email that cites a specific paper of theirs.
- **Oct–Jan is the season.** Most summer-2027 windows close between Oct 15, 2026 and Feb 1, 2027. If you are reading this in March, most summer-2027 portals have closed; still worth checking are Charpak, MATS, LASR and the rolling ones (KAUST, SIPGA, IVISP, CERN Technical).
- **Visa lead times are real.** Schengen: 4–8 weeks in Delhi peak season. Japan CoE: 6–8 weeks. UK: 3+ weeks. US J-1: DS-2019 + interview slot, 2–3 months. Accept an offer, then book the visa appointment the same day.
- **CGPA gates are hard filters, not guidelines.** 8.0/10 (WISE, Khorana), 8.5/10 (Viterbi-IUSSTF), 3.5/4 (KAUST), 3.6/4 (Weizmann), 70% (Mitacs). Below the line = auto-reject regardless of projects.
- **US REUs are mostly closed to non-US students.** NSF-funded REUs, MIT MSRP, Amgen Scholars US, JPL and Fermilab require US citizenship or permanent residency. The US routes that work from India are the IUSSTF programs (Viterbi; Khorana when it runs) and Caltech SURF (campus track). See the [warning section](#what-does-not-work-for-indian-students).

### CERN Summer Student Programme
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=physics,cs,ai,math,ee,mech; region=europe; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Research Internship | **Location:** Geneva, Switzerland | **Funded:** ✅ CHF 94/day net (~CHF 2,800/month, ~₹2.7 lakh) + travel + health insurance

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `~late Jan 2027` (opens ~Dec 2026; CERN says "no later than end of January", so check careers.cern/summer for the exact date) |
| Duration | 8–13 weeks, Jun–Sep 2027 |
| Who | Bachelor's or Master's (not PhD) in physics, engineering, CS or maths; ≥6 semesters completed by summer; still enrolled at program start; never worked at CERN >3 months. All nationalities apply through the main portal (only Canada/Japan/USA have separate routes). India is a CERN Associate Member State. |
| Stipend | CHF 94/calendar day + travel lump sum + insurance; you find your own housing (CERN hostel helps) |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** Attach to an experiment, accelerator, computing or engineering group, do a real project, attend the lecture series and present at the end. It's the most recognised summer line on a physics/CS CV.

**Reality check:** ~300 places for ~5,000+ applicants. Third-year (or later) status is a hard gate. The motivation letter must be specific about what you'd do at CERN. "I love physics" applications go nowhere. Two reference letters are required; line them up in November.

**Apply:** [careers.cern/summer](https://careers.cern/programmes/summer-studentship/)

### CERN Technical Studentship
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=physics,mech,ee,civil,chem,ai,cs,media; region=europe; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=enrolled-only,noc; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Long Internship | **Location:** Geneva, Switzerland | **Funded:** ✅ CHF 3,486/month net (~₹3.3 lakh) + insurance + travel

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `Rolling`, two selection rounds a year (historically ~March and ~September/October); check careers.cern |
| Duration | 4–12 months, fully onsite |
| Who | Nationals of CERN Member or Associate Member States (**India qualifies**); ≥3 semesters completed; enrolled full-time for the whole placement; not a PhD student. Fields: applied physics, mechanical/electrical/civil engineering, materials, data science & AI, IT, HSE, communications. |
| Stipend | CHF 3,486/month + health insurance + 30 days leave + travel expenses |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** The best-paid placement in the Europe section. It's a semester-or-year-long engineering/CS role inside a CERN group, not a summer school. Many Indian students use their final-year project or a gap semester for it.

**Reality check:** You need your institute to approve a 4–12 month absence (NOC/semester-off letter). Engineering profiles with hands-on skills (CAD, FPGA, embedded, DevOps) do better than pure theory.

**Apply:** [careers.cern/technical-studentship](https://careers.cern/programmes/technical-studentship/)

### Max Planck Institutes
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=any; region=europe; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=faculty-first,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Research Internship | **Location:** Germany (80+ institutes) | **Funded:** ✅ Varies (~€800–1,300/month typical; CS internships fully funded)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `Rolling`, faculty-first. The exception is the joint CS internship (MPI for Informatics, MPI-SWS, MPI for Security & Privacy), which closes **Nov 1, 2026** for May–Aug 2027 starts; for other start dates, apply 5+ months ahead. MPI for Intelligent Systems posts its own calls |
| Duration | 2–6 months |
| Who | UG (usually 3rd year+) / MSc; any MPI field (physics, chemistry, bio, CS, neuroscience, law, economics, humanities) |
| Stipend | Set by the institute/PI. The joint CS internship covers a stipend, housing and round-trip travel; MPI-IS internships are paid; elsewhere it's the group's budget (DAAD WISE used to be the usual route from India, but DAAD lists it as unavailable right now) |

**What it is:** No central portal. You identify a group, email the PI or group leader with a specific pitch, and they arrange a "Praktikum" contract. Ask early whether the group can fund you: DAAD WISE, the usual route from India, is listed as unavailable right now (past calls closed ~Nov 1). CS-portal applicants who haven't heard back by Feb 1 should assume it's a no.

## What does NOT work for Indian students

Save yourself the application:

- **NSF-funded REUs (any US university).** Require US citizenship or permanent residency. The same applies to **MIT MSRP**, **Amgen Scholars US** (14 sites, explicitly US citizens/PR), **JPL** (including the Caltech SURF JPL track), **Fermilab/Brookhaven/national-lab SULI**, most **NIH** summer programs.
- **Stanford UGVR.** Only for juniors at select Chinese partner universities (Tsinghua, PKU, USTC, NTU), who apply through their own university. There is no India route, so ignore blog posts saying otherwise.
- **NYU Abu Dhabi summer research.** Open to NYU New York, NYU Shanghai and UAE-based university students only.
- **DAAD RISE Germany** (unless you are enrolled in North America/UK/Ireland) and **RWTH UROP International / TUM PREP** (partner-university exchanges, mostly North American). The India equivalent is DAAD WISE, so check its status.
- **Amgen Scholars Europe / Australia** unless you are enrolled in Europe / Oceania.
- **Imperial UROP** unless you are enrolled at a UK university. From a university outside the UK you qualify only as a UK/Irish national or with EU Settlement Scheme status, and [a UROP can't be used](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/students/undergraduate-research-opportunities-programme/how-to-get-involved/registration/) by anyone who needs a visa to come for it.
- **JSPS Summer Program (Japan)** is only for PhD students from the US, UK, France, Germany, Canada and Sweden.
- **PSI (Switzerland)** unless your institute has a collaboration agreement with PSI and your degree makes the internship compulsory.
- **Anthropic Fellows** unless you already have work rights in the US, UK or Canada, since Anthropic doesn't sponsor visas for fellows.
- **S.N. Bose Scholars**: officially on hold.
- **ISTA ISTernship (Austria)** is [paused](https://phd.pages.ist.ac.at/isternship/) for the 2026 and 2027 cycles. The year-round [scientific internships](https://phd.pages.ista.ac.at/scientific-internships/) are still open: email a group leader, and if they take you, register at least 2 months before you start (pay starts at €1,583 gross/month).
- **India Connect@NTU** is no longer a separate India call. NTU [renamed it](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/about-us/global/global-connect-fellowship) the Global Connect Fellowship and opened it to all countries; apply through [NTU Global Connect](#ntu-global-connect-programme).
- **Canadian NSERC USRA, Australian summer research scholarships, UK UNIQ+/Nuffield**: domestic-student funding.

What *does* work from an Indian campus, ranked by ease: KAUST VSRP → SIPGA/IRIS@NUS → TIIP Taiwan → OIST → Mitacs (B.Tech only) → CERN/DESY → ETH/EPFL/Aalto → Viterbi/Khorana → Weizmann/Caltech (mentor-first).
