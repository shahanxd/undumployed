## How to get invited to launch events in India

Brands in Delhi, Bengaluru and Mumbai fill launch-event guest lists from four pools: media, creators, community superusers, and campus partners. Students can get in through the last three.

1. **Be locally findable.** Set your city on every community profile (Nothing, Snapdragon, Samsung Members, OnePlus). Local invitees are cheaper for brands.
2. **Post before you need to.** Brands check forum/Discord history when picking photowalk attendees. Three months of useful posts beats a week of "pls invite".
3. **Have a shareable portfolio.** A public Instagram/X with device photography or short reviews, even at 500 followers, gets you picked over a blank profile.
4. **Watch the 48-hour windows.** RSVPs close fast. Turn on notifications for: Nothing Discord `#community-events`, Snapdragon Insiders "Opportunities", OnePlus community "Events", Samsung Members "Missions".
5. **Use the campus route.** GDGoC, AWS Cloud Club, Notion and Gemini ambassador leads get campus-partner invites to Google for India, AWS Community Day, Samsung/OnePlus campus activations. Being a chapter lead is the most reliable invite.
6. **Go to the open ones first.** GDG DevFest Delhi/Bengaluru, Google I/O Extended, AWS Community Day, Flutter/Firebase meetups, [Friends of Figma](ambassador-programs.md#friends-of-figma-user-group-leader) meetups and Sony Alpha workshops are open-registration. Brand staff at these events run the closed lists.
7. **Follow the India handles, not global.** Local drops and RSVP calls go out on the India accounts, listed in the [watch list](#watch-list).
8. **Reply to the post, then DM.** For creator/community programs, a public reply to the launch teaser followed by a DM with your portfolio link is how most first invites happen.
9. **Say yes to small things.** Beta-testing surveys, community AMAs, feedback calls. The people who show up to those get the device-loan and launch invites.
10. **Don't pay.** No legitimate brand program charges for an invite or a "creator kit".
