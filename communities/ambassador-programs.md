## Big Tech & AI company programs

### Claude Campus Ambassador (Anthropic)
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd; field=any; region=global; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=enrolled-only,age-limit; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Paid campus ambassador | **Location:** Your campus, worldwide | **Funded:** ✅ US$3,600 stipend (~₹3 lakh) + Anthropic API credits

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Fall 2026 closed Sep 12, 2026 (the window ran Sep 1–12). Next: `~Sep 2027` |
| Duration | Full academic year, Sep 2026 to Jun 2027 |
| Who | 18+, work authorisation in country of study. Three tracks: **Claude Builder Clubs** (undergrads), **Claude Campus Conversations** (master's, STEM or humanities), **Claude Science Workshops** (PhD / postdoc using Claude in research). Grad/PhD tracks pilot from a smaller pool of schools. Past ambassadors must reapply |
| Stipend | US$3,600 cash, API credits for members, Anthropic guest speakers, office visit for some tracks |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** You set up and run a Claude Builder Club (or discussion group / research workshop series) on your campus: workshops, hackathons, demo nights, with Anthropic staff as guest speakers. Free to apply and participate.

**Reality check:** Global, so competitive. Anthropic wants "some level of AI fluency and Claude proficiency" and evidence you can actually gather people. One-line "I like AI" applications lose to "I ran X event with Y attendees". The window is short (12 days in 2026), so have your event record written up before September.

**Apply:** [claude.com/programs/campus](https://claude.com/programs/campus)

### AIESEC
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=any; region=global; funding=unpaid; citizenship=any; gates=age-limit; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Youth-run exchange + membership org | **Location:** 100+ countries; AIESEC in India has local committees in most major cities incl. Delhi | **Funded:** ❌ You pay (exchange program fee + flights; host may cover accommodation/meals)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `Rolling` · membership drives each semester; exchange slots year-round |
| Duration | Global Volunteer 4–8 weeks; Global Talent/Teacher internships 2–12 months |
| Who | 18–30. Membership is for enrolled students; exchanges open to youth |
| Stipend | Global Talent internships are usually paid by the host; Global Volunteer is not |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** Local membership gives leadership roles (sales, marketing, ops) and a big alumni network; exchanges send you abroad on SDG projects or internships.

**Reality check:** Volunteer exchanges cost real money (fee ₹20–45k plus travel). Treat them as a paid experience, not a scholarship. Local membership costs little or nothing, and its leadership roles (recruitment, partnerships, events) are the part worth doing.

**Apply:** [aiesec.org](https://aiesec.org/) · [aiesec.in](https://aiesec.in/)

### Enactus
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=business,econ,policy; region=india; funding=free; citizenship=any; gates=campus-only,team,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Social-enterprise campus team | **Location:** 33 countries incl. India | **Funded:** ❌ Free to join; teams win prize money at national/World Cup competitions

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `Rolling`; join your campus team at the start of the year |
| Duration | Academic year: campus project → Enactus India National Competition → Enactus World Cup. 2026 season: India nationals Sep 25–26 (online), World Cup Nov 16–19 in São Paulo |
| Who | Students at a college with an Enactus team, mostly undergrads. India has 102 active teams, many at Delhi University colleges (SRCC, Hansraj, LSR, Hindu College and others) |
| Stipend | None; project grants via corporate partners |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** Build a real social enterprise with your campus team, then present its results at the Enactus India National Competition. The national champion represents India at the Enactus World Cup.

**Reality check:** Strong for business, economics and social-sector students; less so for pure tech. Impact is judged on outcomes, not decks. Delhi University teams tend to dominate the India rounds, and a year on an Enactus project is a widely respected "worked with real beneficiaries" line on social-sector and consulting applications.

**Apply:** [enactus.org](https://enactus.org/)

Join these on campus; they're the pipeline for most of the programs above.

| **ACM student chapter + ACM India** | Chapter events, ACM India Summer/Winter Schools, student membership perks (see [student perks](../student-perks/README.md)) | Student membership US$19/yr; India pays in INR | Any student | [acm.org/membership/membership-options](https://www.acm.org/membership/membership-options) · [india.acm.org](https://india.acm.org/) |
| **IEEE student branch** | Conferences, IEEE Xtreme, student paper contests, Spectrum | In India (2026): US$14/yr undergrad, US$27/yr grad; half price if you join Mar 1–Aug 15; payable in INR via UPI | Any student | [ieee.org/membership/students](https://www.ieee.org/membership/students/index.html) |

| **TEDx (campus licence)** | Licence to run a TEDx event (standard: ≤100 attendees; larger needs a TED-attendee licence holder) | Free licence; you raise the budget | 18+ organiser; TEDxYouth for schools | [ted.com: organize TEDx](https://www.ted.com/participate/organize-a-local-tedx-event) |
| **Rotaract** | Service projects, Rotary network, RYLA | Club dues | 18+ (upper age limit removed in 2020) | [rotary.org: Rotaract](https://www.rotary.org/en/get-involved/rotaract-clubs) |
| **Toastmasters** | Structured public-speaking pathway | US$144/yr (billed US$72 every six months) + one-time US$25 joining fee; 18% GST on top in India (~₹15k/yr) | 18+ | [toastmasters.org](https://www.toastmasters.org/) |
| **Model UN / IEEE / CSI / IETE / ISTE chapters** | Conference circuit, technical events | Varies | Campus-dependent | Ask your department |

**Indian company "campus ambassador" programs:** Unstop, Internshala and most edtechs run rotating "campus ambassador" drives that are referral-marketing schemes with certificates. Zomato, Swiggy, CRED etc. do not run standing student ambassador programs. If a program's main KPI is signups you drive, it's marketing. That's fine for a business student, but don't confuse it with the funded programs above.
