# Watchlist

> Where we look for new entries, and for [drops](../drops.md) above all: one-off hackathons, sprints and cohorts that are announced a few weeks ahead and gone a month later. These are **sources, never apply links**. Every entry still links the organiser's own page and has to clear the [quality bar](../CONTRIBUTING.md#drops-one-off-opportunities).

Checked on the 1st and 15th of each month. Add a source here when a good drop turns up somewhere we weren't looking.

---

## Hackathons and build challenges

| Source | What turns up there | Notes |
|--------|---------------------|-------|
| [Devpost](https://devpost.com/hackathons) | Company-run online hackathons (Google, Meta, Amazon, AWS, NVIDIA) | Sort by prize. Read the rules page for country exclusions and the 18+ line. |
| [Kaggle competitions](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions) | Google/DeepMind and lab-sponsored ML competitions | "Featured" and "Research" tiers carry the real money. Note the entry deadline as well as the final one. |
| [MLH season calendar](https://www.mlh.com/seasons/2027/events) | Student hackathons with MLH sponsor prizes | Mostly recurring college events; the one-offs are the sponsored "global hack weeks". |
| [Devfolio](https://devfolio.co/hackathons) | Indian hackathons, Web3 and AI | Many are college-run. Check for an outside sponsor before it counts. |
| [lablab.ai](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons) | AI hackathons backed by AMD, Google and model labs | Frequent. Only the ones with ≥$5k and a known sponsor get in. Check India eligibility, which the pages often leave out. |
| [Apart Research sprints](https://apartresearch.com/sprints) | Weekend AI-safety research hackathons | Listed as a recurring entry in [hackathons](../hackathons/README.md#apart-research-sprints-ai-safety); individual sprints don't need their own drop. |
| [ETHGlobal](https://ethglobal.com/events) | Ethereum hackathons, online and in cities | Online ones are the realistic route from India. |
| [Hack2skill](https://hack2skill.com/) | Google India programs, government challenges | |
| [Unstop](https://unstop.com/hackathons) | Indian corporate hackathons | Mostly a lead: find the company's own page. |

## Events, sprints and short programs

| Source | What turns up there | Notes |
|--------|---------------------|-------|
| [Luma](https://luma.com/discover) | AI meetups, kickoffs and one-day hackathons, by city | Bengaluru and Delhi calendars. Lab-run events often only exist here. |
| [AISafety.com training](https://aisafety.com/training) | AI-safety courses, camps and fellowships | Good for one-off cohorts. |
| [80,000 Hours job board](https://jobs.80000hours.org/) | Fellowships and research programs with funding | Filter for "fellowship" and "internship". |

## Accounts to watch on X

Labs and companies announce one-offs here first, often two or three weeks before anything else lists them: [@kaggle](https://x.com/kaggle), [@googlegemma](https://x.com/googlegemma), [@huggingface](https://x.com/huggingface), [@cohere](https://x.com/cohere), [@Lexsi_labs](https://x.com/Lexsi_labs), [@MLHacks](https://x.com/MLHacks), [@devfolio](https://x.com/devfolio), [@lablabai](https://x.com/lablabai), [@ETHGlobal](https://x.com/ETHGlobal), [@apartresearch](https://x.com/apartresearch).

A post is a lead, not a source. Open the organiser's page, check the dates, eligibility and entry fee there, and link that.
