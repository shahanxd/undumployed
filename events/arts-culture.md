### Tomorrowland Belgium 2026
> **Dates:** Weekend 1: Jul 17–19 | Weekend 2: Jul 24–26, 2026 | **Location:** Boom, Belgium

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Cost | ~€250–400 weekend pass. Dreamville camping: €500+ |
| Visa | Schengen required |
| Access | Pre-registration required — sells via lottery |

**What it is:** THE electronic music festival. Insane stage production, global DJ lineup, dreamlike atmosphere. Nothing in the world compares for pure spectacle. Pre-register on Tomorrowland account for ticket access — extremely high demand.

**Apply:** [tomorrowland.com](https://www.tomorrowland.com) — pre-register for ticket access

### Tour de France 2026
> **Dates:** July 4–26, 2026 | **Route:** Grand Départ in Barcelona → Paris finish

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Cost | **FREE** — watch from the roadside |
| Visa | Schengen (France/Spain) |

**What it is:** The most iconic cycling race. Free to watch from public roads. Carnival atmosphere — the publicity caravan throws merchandise, mountain stages have festivals. Alpe d'Huez stage is legendary.

**Tip:** For mountain stages, arrive 3–4 days early. Best spots fill fast.

### Burning Man 2026
> **Dates:** August 30 – September 7, 2026 | **Location:** Black Rock City, Nevada, USA | **Theme:** Axis Mundi

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Cost | ~$575 per ticket + vehicle pass. Total trip: $1,500–3,000+ |
| Visa | US B1/B2 required |

**What it is:** Not a festival — a temporary city built on radical self-expression. Art installations, mutant vehicles, no commerce inside. Transformative and extreme. Radical self-reliance required — extreme desert conditions.

**Apply:** [burningman.org](https://burningman.org) — multiple ticket sale phases including lottery
