### CES 2027
> **Dates:** January 6–9, 2027 | **Location:** Las Vegas Convention Center + citywide | **Access:** Trade/Industry only

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Cost | ~$300–500 for verified industry attendees |
| Visa | US B1/B2 — apply 4–6 months early |
| Register | Registration expected to open ~Sep 2026. Watch ces.tech. |

**What it is:** The world's largest consumer tech trade show. 60th anniversary edition. Every major tech company showcases here — AI, robotics, EVs, smart home, health tech. Unmatched for seeing what's coming 12–18 months out.

**Access note:** NOT open to general public. Must prove industry affiliation (business card, company website, press credentials, or professional invoice).

**Apply:** [ces.tech](https://www.ces.tech)
