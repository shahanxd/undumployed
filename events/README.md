**Visa:** the destination-by-destination table lives in [guides/visas-for-indian-passport.md](../guides/visas-for-indian-passport.md). Short version: a valid US/UK/Schengen visa unlocks conditional visa-free entry to Taiwan, Türkiye e-visa and several others; US B1/B2 interview waits in India are 7–12 months, so plan a year ahead for US events.

**Money:** most research conferences have student volunteer programs (free registration) and travel grants; most industry conferences have a free virtual tier. Student-specific funding is in [fellowships/research-and-networking.md](../fellowships/research-and-networking.md#travel-grants-for-conferences).
