## How student funding works

- **Volunteer programs** (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR/ICCV, CHI, SIGGRAPH, AAAI, KDD, ACL family): you do ~10–20 hours of badge-scanning/session help, registration is waived, sometimes hotel is shared. Apply 3–5 months before the conference; slots go fast.
- **Financial assistance / travel grants** (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, USENIX, IEEE S&P/CCS, ACM SIGs, IEEE RAS, APS, AGU): usually need-based, priority to first authors and underrepresented groups. Some pay cash ($500–2,000); others cover only registration + hotel (NeurIPS). Applications usually close 6–9 weeks before the event.
- **Author?** Acceptance is not subsidised. It usually means at least one author must register (some conferences require the full, non-student rate) and present in person. Many Indian institutes (IITs, IISc, IIITs) plus [ANRF (ex-SERB) ITS](../fellowships/research-and-networking.md#anrf-international-travel-support-its), ACM India, Microsoft Research India and Google India have separate travel grants for student authors. Ask your advisor and department office first.
- **Visa reality:** US/Canada visa slots are the bottleneck. For anything in the USA, apply the day you get acceptance. Japan, Korea, Hong Kong, Greece, Czechia are more forgiving.
