# Events: Tech & Developer (Global)

> Big tech shows, developer conferences, hacker cons, open-source summits and crypto/AI gatherings worldwide, Oct 2026 → Dec 2027. Free-virtual and student/volunteer routes flagged wherever they exist.

**Jump to:** [At a glance](#at-a-glance) · [Oct–Dec 2026](#octdec-2026) · [Jan–Mar 2027](#janmar-2027) · [Apr–Jun 2027](#aprjun-2027) · [Jul–Sep 2027](#julsep-2027) · [Oct–Dec 2027 + recurring](#octdec-2027--recurring) · [Ended / replaced](#ended--replaced)

**Visas (Indian passport):** full table in [guides/visas-for-indian-passport.md](../guides/visas-for-indian-passport.md). US B1/B2: first-time interview waits are ~7–12 months, so plan a year ahead or use the free virtual tracks. Schengen: apply 8–10 weeks out. Qatar: visa-free for 30 days. Taiwan: free online TAC if you hold, or held in the last 10 years, a US/UK/Schengen/Japan visa. UAE: e-visa (2–5 working days); visa-on-arrival is only certain with a US Green Card or UK/EU residence permit.
