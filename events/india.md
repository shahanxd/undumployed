### Bharat Mobility / Auto Expo 2027
> **Dates:** February 4–9, 2027 | **Location:** Bharat Mandapam (Pragati Maidan) + Yashobhoomi (Dwarka), New Delhi

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Cost | ~₹200–500 (historically very affordable) |
| Visa | None — you're home |

**What it is:** India's biggest auto and mobility show. EV launches, concept vehicles, two-wheelers, commercial vehicles, future mobility tech. If you're in Delhi — this is a zero-effort must-attend. Hundreds of brand launches over 6 days.

**Apply:** [bharat-mobility.com](https://www.bharat-mobility.com) — registration opens closer to event

### Ziro Festival of Music 2026
> **Dates:** September 23–27, 2026 | **Location:** Ziro Valley, Arunachal Pradesh

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Cost | ~₹5,000–8,000 (camping included) |
| Permit | Inner Line Permit required for Arunachal Pradesh — free, apply online |

**What it is:** India's most beautiful music festival. Rice paddies, mountains, indie and folk music, Apatani tribal culture. Intimate (3,000–5,000 people) and genuinely magical.

**Apply:** [zirofestival.com](https://www.zirofestival.com)
