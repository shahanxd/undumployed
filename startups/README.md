> Money, mentorship and cover for people who want to build something: talent investors that need no idea, accelerators, government-of-India grants, student startup competitions and builder fellowships. Indian citizens can apply to almost everything here; the few US-only ones are flagged.

## Talent investors and no-idea-needed

### Entrepreneur First (EF) Bangalore
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=any; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Talent investor | **Location:** Bengaluru (FORM) → San Francisco (LAUNCH) | **Funded:** ✅ Living stipend during FORM + pre-seed investment if your company passes Investment Committee

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Oct 5, 2026** early deadline for first selection round; two more rounds follow (dates on the portal) |
| Duration | Winter 27 cohort starts Jan–Feb 2027; full-time, in person; ~3-month FORM then LAUNCH |
| Who | Exceptional individuals: strong researchers, engineers, ex-founders, top competitive programmers. No idea, no co-founder, no company required. Final-year students who can go full-time at start are fine. |
| Stipend | Monthly grant during FORM (previously ~₹3.6L over the phase, but confirm the current figure on the portal); investment up to ~$250k on company formation |
| Stage | UG-final / PG / PhD / working |

**What it is:** EF pays you to find a co-founder and an idea inside a cohort, then invests if the pairing works. Winter cohorts are the ones that start in January; Fall cohort applications are separate.

**Reality check:** Selection screens for "proof of exceptional" (publications, olympiad/ICPC ranks, shipped products, prior founding). Expect a short form, a screening call and a partner interview. Dropout rate during FORM is high by design; not everyone leaves with a funded company.

**Apply:** [apply.joinef.com/app/bangalore-winter](https://apply.joinef.com/app/bangalore-winter/)

### Antler India Residency
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=any; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Talent investor / residency | **Location:** Bengaluru (in person) | **Funded:** ✅ One-time ₹2L grant + standard deal ~₹4Cr (~$425k) for 11% equity

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Rolling; cohorts start several times a year (see "Cohort start dates" on the India page). Last announced AI Residency ran Feb 2026 with a 3-week sprint. |
| Duration | Residency weeks + follow-on; funding decisions in ~3 weeks |
| Who | Solo founders or teams, pre-idea to early prototype; no incorporation needed to apply |
| Stipend | ₹2L expense grant, free office, $1M+ perks; investment only if you pass the IC |
| Stage | UG-final / PG / working |

**What it is:** Antler's India arm backs "obsessed outliers" before anything is obvious: you join a residency, find a co-founder if needed, and pitch for the standard cheque.

**Reality check:** 11% for ~₹4Cr is a heavier dilution than YC/SPC; the value is the speed and co-founder pool. Most residents do not get funded.

**Apply:** [antler.co/location/india](https://www.antler.co/location/india)

## Accelerators and seed programs

### Y Combinator (YC)
<!-- tags: stage=hs,ug,pg,phd,grad; field=any; region=north-america; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Accelerator | **Location:** San Francisco (3 months, in person) | **Funded:** ✅ $500,000 ($125k for 7% + $375k uncapped MFN SAFE) + $12M+ in credits

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Nov 2, 2026, 8pm PT** for Winter 2027 (decisions by Dec 11). Late applications still read. Four batches a year (Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall). |
| Duration | 3 months + Demo Day |
| Who | Any startup, any stage, any country; solo founders accepted. **Early Decision for students:** apply now, select "a batch after Winter 2027", get funded now and join after graduating. |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD / any |

**What it is:** The default accelerator. Indian alumni include Razorpay, Meesho, Groww, Zepto. Early Decision is the student-specific hook: an offer in hand while you finish your degree.

**Reality check:** ~1–2% acceptance. Traction or a demo-able product helps enormously; idea-only applications from students usually need a very strong team story. Interview is 10 minutes.

**Apply:** [ycombinator.com/apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply) · [Early Decision](https://www.ycombinator.com/early-decision)
