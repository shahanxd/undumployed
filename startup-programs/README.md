### Y Combinator (YC)
> **Type:** Accelerator | **Location:** San Francisco, USA | **Funded:** ✅ $500,000 per company

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Rolling year-round. W27/S27 early decision available now. |
| Duration | 3-month program (in-person, SF) |
| Who | Any startup, anywhere. Indian founders welcomed — Zepto, Groww, Meesho, Razorpay are alumni. |

**What it is:** The world's most famous startup accelerator. $500k standard deal. Demo Day at end of batch connects you to hundreds of investors.

**Reality check:** You need a product or startup idea — ideally with early traction. Individuals without a project or co-founder are not the target. Apply early decision for W27/S27 for faster response.

**Apply:** [ycombinator.com/apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply) — rolling basis year-round

### Entrepreneur First (EF) India
> **Type:** Talent investor | **Location:** Bangalore, India → San Francisco | **Funded:** ✅ ₹3,60,000 grant + up to $250,000 investment

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Early deadline: October 5, 2026** (Winter 2027 cohort). Program starts February 2027. |
| Duration | 12-week FORM phase (Bangalore) → LAUNCH phase (San Francisco) if company formed |
| Who | Exceptional individuals — builders, researchers, engineers, ex-founders. **No startup idea required.** |

**What it is:** EF backs exceptional people *before* they have a startup idea. They facilitate co-founder matching and idea development. If your company forms and gets Investment Committee approval → up to $250k. Then move to San Francisco for Demo Day.

**Reality check:** They are looking for genuine outliers. Research publications, top competitive programming ranking, significant open source contributions, ex-founding experience — these are the profiles that get in. Being smart and hard-working is not enough.

**Prep steps:**
1. Identify your strongest "proof of exceptional" — what have you done that 99% of applicants haven't?
2. Apply via joinef.com — the application is short but the screening is rigorous
3. Early deadline (Oct 5) gives priority consideration

**Apply:** [joinef.com](https://www.joinef.com)

### Peak XV Surge
> **Type:** Investment | **Location:** India / Southeast Asia | **Funded:** ✅ $500k–$5M seed capital

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Rolling year-round |
| Who | Any startup at any stage including idea stage. No geographic restriction within India/SEA. |

**What it is:** Early-stage startup investment program by Peak XV Partners (formerly Sequoia). Not a fellowship — this is a VC investment. 25 minutes to apply; response within 2–3 weeks.

**Reality check:** You need a startup concept, not just an idea on paper. They invest in companies, not individuals.

**Apply:** [surgeahead.com](https://www.surgeahead.com)

### Kleiner Perkins Fellows
> **Type:** Internship program | **Location:** Silicon Valley, USA | **Funded:** ✅ Paid internship

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | ~January–February each year |
| Duration | 10–12 weeks (summer) |
| Who | University students. Engineering, Product, or Design track. Must be able to do in-person in Silicon Valley. |

**What it is:** Internship at KP portfolio companies (Figma, Stripe, Rippling, etc.). Operated by Kleiner Perkins — strong VC network access as a bonus.

**Reality check:** Requires being in Bay Area in person for the summer. Not viable if you can't get a US visa and relocate.

**Apply:** [kleinerperkins.com/fellows](https://kleinerperkins.com/fellows)
