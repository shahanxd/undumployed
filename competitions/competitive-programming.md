## The India pathway: ZCO → INOI → IOITC → IOI

ZCO (coding) or ZIO (pen-and-paper), `~Nov–Dec 2026` → INOI, `~Jan–Feb 2027` → IOI Training Camp (IOITC, top ~25–30, `~May–Jun 2027`) → India team of 4 at IOI 2027.

### Indian Computing Olympiad (ZCO / ZIO / INOI)
<!-- tags: stage=hs; field=cs; region=india; funding=prize; citizenship=indian-only; gates=enrolled-only; format=hybrid -->
> **Type:** National informatics olympiad (IARCS) | **Location:** Centres across India / online for ZCO | **Funded:** ✅ IOI team fully funded; no cash

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | ICO-2027 registration opens **Oct 2026** on iarcs.org.in. Expected: ZCO/ZIO `~late Nov–Dec 2026`, INOI `~Jan–Feb 2027`. |
| Who | Any student registered in school up to class 12 in 2026-27 (no minimum age; you're ineligible once you've finished class 12). Small registration fee. |
| Stage | HS |

**What it is:** Two doors into INOI: ZCO (write code in C++/Python/Java) or ZIO (pen-and-paper algorithmic reasoning). INOI's top ~25–30 go to IOITC; the top 4 after camp go to IOI. An IOI medal carries real weight in CS admissions worldwide and in the olympiad-based admission routes at IIIT-H, CMI and several IITs.

**Reality check:** INOI is Codeforces Div 2 D/E level. Kids who start at Codeforces 1600+ in class 9 make IOITC; starting in class 11 with zero background rarely does. Class 12 board year conflicts with INOI in January, so plan for it.

**Apply:** [iarcs.org.in/inoi](https://www.iarcs.org.in/inoi/) · training: [USACO Guide](https://usaco.guide/)

## Team & school contests

### ICPC India regionals 2026-27
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=cs; region=india; funding=unpaid; citizenship=any; gates=team,enrolled-only,age-limit; format=hybrid -->
> **Type:** Team programming contest (3 per team) | **Location:** India regional sites (Amritapuri, Chennai, Kanpur, Mathura) → Asia West Championship → World Finals | **Funded:** ❌ Regionals: registration fee (varies by site) and self-funded travel; ✅ World Finals travel covered by ICPC/sponsors

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | 2026-27 season. Team registration closed **Sep 25, 2026** for Amritapuri, Kanpur and Mathura. Chennai's host page lists Sep 30, but icpc.global and indiaicpc.in give earlier dates, so don't count on it. A team can enter at most two sites, each with its own registration and ₹1,500 prelim fee. One online prelim for all four sites: **Oct 3, 2026**. Onsite: Chennai (SRMIST) `~Dec 10–12, 2026`; Kanpur multi-site (Kanpur · Gandhinagar · Pune) **Dec 22–23, 2026**; Mathura (GLA University) **Dec 27–28, 2026**; Amritapuri multi-site (Kollam · Bengaluru · Coimbatore · Mysuru) **Jan 1–2, 2027**. Asia West Championship `~Mar 2027`; World Finals 2027 `TBA`. Next: `~Jun–Sep 2027` registration for 2027-28. |
| Who | University students enrolled at least half-time who meet ICPC's years-of-study/age rule and appearance limits (exact cut-offs in the current rules on icpc.global); teams of 3 from the same institution + a coach; any branch |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** The premier team algorithmic contest. In India, one online prelim is shared by all four sites, and each site shortlists its own onsite teams from those registered with it. A handful of onsite winners advance to the Asia West Championship, and 1–3 Indian teams reach the World Finals each year.

**Reality check:** Onsite qualification from a Tier-2/3 college is realistic; WF is not without a 2000+ Codeforces team. Multi-site registration (Amritapuri + one more) doubles your onsite chances. Fees are ₹ few thousand per team per site.

**Apply:** [icpc.global](https://icpc.global/) (team registration) · [indiaicpc.in](https://indiaicpc.in/) (all four India sites: dates, fees, rules)

## Annual company contests

### Meta Hacker Cup
<!-- tags: stage=hs,ug,pg,phd,grad; field=cs; region=remote; funding=prize; citizenship=any; gates=; format=remote -->
> **Type:** Individual algorithmic contest | **Location:** Online (the final was online in 2024 and 2025) | **Funded:** ✅ Cash prizes for the top 25 ($20,000 for 1st, down to $200 for 16th–25th); T-shirts for the top 2,000 in Round 2

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `~Oct–Dec 2026`, going by 2025: registration opened Oct 3; practice round Oct 10 (5 days); Round 1 Oct 18 (open to all, top 5,000 advance); Round 2 Nov 15; Round 3 Nov 29 (top 25 go to the final); online final Dec 20. 2025 had no separate qualification round, and registration stays open until the end of Round 1. |
| Who | Anyone, any age/country; individual |
| Stage | HS / UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** Meta's flagship contest since 2011. Round 2 top-2000 t-shirt is a common "first CP achievement"; Round 3 (top 500) gets recruiter attention.

**Reality check:** The early rounds let thousands through (the top 5,000 from Round 1 advanced in 2025); Round 2 is the real filter. You run your code on your own machine and upload the output within a short window after downloading the full input, so test on large cases before you download.

**Apply:** [facebook.com/codingcompetitions/hacker-cup](https://www.facebook.com/codingcompetitions/hacker-cup)

### Codeforces
<!-- tags: stage=hs,ug,pg,phd,grad; field=cs; region=remote; funding=prize; citizenship=any; gates=; format=remote -->
> **Type:** Rated contests | **Location:** Online | **Funded:** ✅ Occasional prizes in Global Rounds / sponsored rounds; otherwise free

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `Rolling`: Div 1/2/3/4 rounds several times a week (usually 20:05 IST), Educational rounds, Global Rounds |
| Who | Anyone |
| Stage | HS / UG / PG |

**What it is:** The rating that recruiters and ICPC coaches actually look at. Rough mapping: 1400 (Specialist) = interview-ready DSA; 1900 (Candidate Master) = ICPC regional onsite contender; 2100+ = INOI/IOITC-level for school students.

## How to train

1. **Ladder:** AtCoder ABC (A–D) → Codeforces Div 3/4 → Div 2 → Educational rounds. Two rated contests a week, every week, for a year beats any course.
2. **Upsolve** every contest: the problem you failed is the one you learn from.
3. **Read:** [CP-Algorithms](https://cp-algorithms.com/), [USACO Guide](https://usaco.guide/) (free, structured, olympiad-oriented), Codeforces EDU.
4. **Team practice for ICPC:** Universal Cup mirrors + Codeforces Gym (old regionals) on one machine, 5 hours, no internet.
5. **School students:** work through the INOI past papers on [iarcs.org.in/inoi](https://www.iarcs.org.in/inoi/).
6. **Ignore** paid "CP bootcamps". Everything above is free.
