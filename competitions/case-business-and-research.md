## Business & entrepreneurship

### Hult Prize
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd; field=any; region=global; funding=prize; citizenship=any; gates=team,enrolled-only; format=hybrid -->
> **Type:** Global student social-enterprise competition | **Location:** Campus → national → Ashridge House (London) accelerator → global finals | **Funded:** ✅ $1M seed investment to the winning team, in exchange for a warrant for 10% of the company's equity; accelerator is fully funded (travel + stay)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **2027 cycle:** Stage 1 Qualifiers **Sep 2026–Feb 2027** (on-campus event run by a student Campus Director, *or* open application if your campus has none); Stage 2 Nationals Apr–May 2027; Stage 3 Digital Incubator Jun–Jul; Stage 4 Global Accelerator Aug 2027 (London); Stage 5 Finals Sep 2027 |
| Duration | 12 months |
| Who | Teams of 2–4 students enrolled in a degree program of at least two years (undergraduate, master's or PhD, any country); every member must be 18 or older by Feb 28, 2027. One founder must remain a student through the cycle |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** The world's largest student startup competition, with 200,000+ participants a year. Teams build for-profit startups that advance at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal, around an annual theme. Indian campuses (IITs, IIMs, DU, VIT, SRM, Manipal…) run some of the biggest on-campus rounds.

**Reality check:** On-campus and national rounds are winnable with a solid pitch; the accelerator is a real 5-week program with 60 teams; $1M goes to one team, as an investment for 10% equity rather than a grant. For most teams the value is the campus round and the mentoring. Become the Campus Director if your college has none. It's a résumé line by itself.

**Apply:** [hultprize.org/register](https://www.hultprize.org/register)

### GSEA (Global Student Entrepreneur Awards)
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=business; region=global; funding=prize; citizenship=any; gates=enrolled-only,work-experience; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Competition for students running a real business | **Location:** India city rounds → national → global finals | **Funded:** ✅ Local cash prizes, a US$100,000 global finals pool (US$50,000 for first) and a funded trip to the global final for the national winner

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Varies by city. The 2026–27 season opened Jul 1, 2026, and the India rounds close between **Oct 15, 2026** (Gurgaon, Kerala) and **Jan 15, 2027** (Uttar Pradesh). EO lists these dates as anticipated, so check your city’s listing. Global finals ~Jun 2027 |
| Who | Enrolled UG/PG students aged 18–30 who own a business that has run for 6+ consecutive months, with US$500+ in revenue or US$1,000+ raised. One founder per company. Rounds are run by Entrepreneurs' Organization (EO) chapters, and Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad have none this season, so pick “Other” on the application if your city isn't listed |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** The Entrepreneurs' Organization's competition for students who already run a business. You pitch at a local round run by an EO chapter (15 Indian cities and states hold them, mostly Nov 2026–Jan 2027), and winners advance to the national and then the global finals.

**Reality check:** You need an operating business with revenue or investment behind it; an idea or a pitch deck doesn't qualify.

**Apply:** [eonetwork.org/gsea/apply](https://eonetwork.org/gsea/apply/)
