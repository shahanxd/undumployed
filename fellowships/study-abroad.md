## Prestige leadership fellowships

### Rhodes Scholarship (India)
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,grad; field=any; region=uk; funding=full; citizenship=indian-only; gates=age-limit; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Scholarship for any Oxford postgraduate degree | **Location:** Oxford, UK | **Funded:** ✅ Full (fees + ~£19k/yr stipend + flights + visa/health surcharge)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Next: `~Jun 1–Jul 23, 2027` (2027-entry round opened Jun 1, closed **Jul 23, 2026**; references Aug 6) |
| Duration | 2 years (extendable to 3 for DPhil) |
| Who | Indian citizen; 18–23 on Oct 1 of application year (≤27 if you finished UG late); bachelor's with first class; any field |
| Stipend | Full fees + living stipend + two economy flights + settling-in allowance |
| Stage | Final-year UG / recent grad |

**What it is:** Five Rhodes Scholarships for India per year, for any full-time Oxford postgraduate course (MSc, MPhil, BCL, DPhil, second BA). Selection is on academics, "energy to use one's talents to the full", moral courage and leadership. The interview weekend in Delhi is the real filter.

**Reality check:** Five seats nationally. Winners usually have a first-class degree from a top institution plus a distinct sustained commitment (research, public service, sport, arts). Start assembling referees (5–8 needed) in spring 2027, not July.

**Apply:** [rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/scholarships/applications/india](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/scholarships/applications/india/)

### Gates Cambridge Scholarship
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=any; region=uk; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Scholarship for a PhD, MPhil or 1-year master's | **Location:** University of Cambridge, UK | **Funded:** ✅ Full

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Dec 8, 2026** or **Jan 6, 2027** at 11:59 PM UK, whichever is your course's funding deadline (check the Course Directory) |
| Duration | Full course length |
| Who | Citizens of any country except the UK; applying for a full-time Cambridge postgraduate course; outstanding academics + leadership + commitment to improving lives of others |
| Stipend | University fees + £21,000+/yr maintenance + one return flight + visa + immigration health surcharge; discretionary academic development, family and hardship funding |
| Stage | PG / PhD |

**What it is:** ~80 scholars/year worldwide (about 55 non-US, 25 US), funded by the Gates Foundation's $210M endowment. You tick a box in the Cambridge Graduate Application Portal and write a Gates statement; the department must first rank you academically before the Gates panel sees you.

**Reality check:** Success rate ~1–2%. Departments shortlist first on pure academics, so a below-top-tier transcript never reaches the Gates interview. Strongest for PhD applicants with publications and a clear research plan. Indian scholars are selected every year, often from IITs/IISc/DU/TIFR and from public-health, development, and engineering backgrounds.

**Apply:** [gatescambridge.org/apply](https://www.gatescambridge.org/apply/)

> **Type:** Fellowship for a 1-year Master's in Global Affairs | **Location:** Tsinghua University, Beijing | **Funded:** ✅ Full

|-------|---------|

<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=any; region=north-america; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=age-limit; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Scholarship for any Stanford graduate degree + leadership program | **Location:** Stanford, USA | **Funded:** ✅ Full (up to 3 years)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|

### Chevening Scholarship
<!-- tags: stage=grad; field=any; region=uk; funding=full; citizenship=indian-only; gates=work-experience; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Scholarship for a 1-year UK master's | **Location:** Any UK university | **Funded:** ✅ Full

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Oct 6, 2026, 11:00 UTC (4:30 PM IST)**; the application system locks after this |
| Duration | 1-year master's, Sep/Oct 2027 start |
| Who | Indian citizen; 2,800+ hours (≈2 years) of work experience gained after your bachelor's, by the time you apply; undergraduate degree equivalent to UK 2:1; must return to India for 2 years after; apply to 3 UK courses, get 1 unconditional offer by Jul 2027 |
| Stipend | Full tuition + monthly stipend + economy flights + arrival allowance + visa + TB test + thesis grant |
| Stage | PG (post-work) |

**What it is:** The UK government's flagship award; India is one of its largest cohorts (typically 60–100). Timeline: reading committees Oct–Jan, shortlist mid-Feb 2027, interviews at British High Commission/Deputy High Commissions Mar–Apr 2027, results from Jun 2027. India also has partner awards (e.g., Chevening–Oxford Centre for Islamic Studies, some university co-funded awards) that appear inside the same application.

**Reality check:** The 2,800-hour rule is strict, and only work done after your bachelor's counts, so college internships don't. After graduation, full-time, part-time, voluntary work and paid or unpaid internships all count. Four essays (leadership, networking, study in UK, career plan) decide the shortlist. Generic "UK has good universities" essays fail.

**Apply:** [chevening.org/scholarship/india](https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/india/)

### Rotary Peace Fellowship
<!-- tags: stage=grad; field=policy,med; region=global; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=work-experience,nomination; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Fellowship for a master's (15–24 months) or 1-year professional certificate | **Location:** Rotary Peace Centres (Uppsala, Bradford, ICU Tokyo, Queensland, Duke/UNC; certificate at Chulalongkorn, Makerere, Bahçeşehir) | **Funded:** ✅ Full

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `~May 15, 2027` (annual; Rotary district endorsement needed first, so start by Mar 2027) |
| Duration | Master's 15–24 months / certificate 1 year |
| Who | Master's: bachelor's + 3+ yrs relevant full-time work in peace/development/humanitarian fields; certificate: 5+ yrs; proficiency in English; district endorsement |

## Removed this cycle

- **Orange Tulip Scholarship (Netherlands):** discontinued. Nuffic no longer runs it, and [Study in NL's scholarship page](https://www.studyinnl.org/finances/scholarships) now lists only the NL Scholarship, Erasmus+ and MSP. The [NL Scholarship](https://www.studyinnl.org/finances/nl-scholarship) (€5,000 in the first year, non-EEA students) is the closest replacement.
- **Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships:** discontinued after the 2024 competition; the University of Toronto's graduate school lists it as discontinued. Its replacement, the Canada Graduate Research Scholarship (Doctoral), only takes international students already enrolled in a Canadian PhD.
- **Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships:** discontinued after the 2024 competition; the University of Toronto and UBC list it as discontinued. The postdoc successor is the Canada Postdoctoral Research Award.
- **Stanford Reliance Dhirubhai Fellowship (MBA):** no longer offered. The last application window ran from Apr 20 to May 22, 2020, and Stanford GSB's aid pages no longer list it; Indian admits can still get [GSB's need-based fellowships](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/programs/mba/tuition-financial-aid).
- **Hubert H. Humphrey Fellowship:** a mid-career program that needs 5+ years of full-time professional experience, so it isn't open to students. Details at [usief.org.in](https://www.usief.org.in/fulbright-fellowships/fellowships-for-indian-citizen/hubert-h-humphrey-fellowship-program/).
