### Polaris Fellowship
> **Type:** Fellowship | **Location:** Bangalore, India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹6,00,000 stipend + ₹4,00,000 tech credits

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | ~June 2027 (2026 cohort closed Jun 28, 2026 — watch for 2027) |
| Duration | 6 months residential (Bangalore) |
| Class size | 10 students nationwide |
| Who | UG, PG, or PhD students + recent graduates. No CGPA cutoff — pure proof of work. |

**What it is:** India's most elite startup/tech fellowship. 6 months in Bangalore working alongside founders and engineering teams at top startups and VC firms. 10 spots per cohort, nationwide.

**Reality check:** No CGPA requirement — they care about what you've built, shipped, or researched. GitHub, open source contributions, research papers, startup experience all count. Generic applicants with good grades and no track record don't get in.

**Prep steps:**
1. Build something public and linkable (GitHub project, research paper, a product people use)
2. Document your proof of work clearly in your application
3. Watch fellowship.polariscampus.com for 2027 cohort announcement

**Apply:** [fellowship.polariscampus.com](https://fellowship.polariscampus.com)

### PMRF
> **Type:** Fellowship — PhD | **Location:** IITs, IISc, IISERs | **Funded:** ✅ ₹70,000–80,000/month + ₹2L/year research grant

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Twice/year: ~May and December |
| Duration | Full PhD duration |
| Who | **Cannot apply directly — your institute nominates you.** B.Tech/Int. M.Tech/M.Sc from IITs/NITs/IISERs/IISc with CGPA 8.0+, pursuing PhD at a PMRF-granting institute. |

**What it is:** Prime Minister's Research Fellowship — the most prestigious doctoral fellowship in India. Monthly stipend starts at ₹70k and increases each year.

**Reality check:** Institution nomination required. If you're eligible (8.0+ CGPA at an eligible institute, going for PhD), tell your faculty supervisor and department — they handle the nomination.

**Apply:** [pmrf.in](https://www.pmrf.in) — through your institute

### INSPIRE SHE
> **Type:** Scholarship — Undergraduate | **Location:** India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹80,000/year (₹60k scholarship + ₹20k summer research grant)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Annual (check online-inspire.gov.in) |
| Duration | Full UG duration |
| Who | Top 1% Class XII boards, OR top 10,000 JEE Advanced rank, OR NEET top rankers, OR NTSE/International Olympiad medallists — enrolled in B.Sc./B.S./Int. M.Sc. in **natural sciences** (not engineering). |

**What it is:** DST's replacement for KVPY. Scholarship for undergraduate students in basic sciences. The ₹20k summer grant is specifically for research experience.

**Reality check:** Eligibility is automatic if you meet the criteria — you don't "win" this through an essay. Check the government portal to confirm eligibility and apply.

**Apply:** [online-inspire.gov.in](https://online-inspire.gov.in)

### Wellcome-DBT India Alliance
> **Type:** Fellowship — Research | **Location:** India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹80–90 lakh total over 5 years (salary + research + overhead)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Annual — typically closes March |
| Duration | 5 years |
| Who | PhD holders (0–7 years post-PhD) in biomedical, public health, or clinical research. For those establishing independent labs — not for students. |

**What it is:** Early Career Fellowship to set up an independent research lab in India. Fully funded 5-year program in partnership with Wellcome Trust (UK) and DBT (India).

**Reality check:** This is for PhD holders establishing labs, not students. Included here because PG/PhD track students should know it exists for their next stage.

**Apply:** [indiaalliance.org](https://indiaalliance.org) → Fellowships
