# Fellowships & Scholarships: India

> Indian government, corporate, foundation and social-sector money for Indian students, from school to PhD, plus the big post-college fellowships (YIF, LAMP, Teach For India, Gandhi, SBI YFI, CM fellowships). Study-abroad funding is in [study-abroad.md](study-abroad.md); paid research summers are in [internships/research-india.md](../internships/research-india.md).

**Jump to:** [School level (Class 9–12)](#school-level-class-912) · [Undergraduate & PG scholarships](#undergraduate--pg-scholarships) · [Post-college fellowships (1–2 years)](#post-college-fellowships-12-years) · [Government & state fellowships](#government--state-fellowships) · [Women-specific & inclusion](#women-specific--inclusion) · [PhD & research funding](#phd--research-funding)

**Status notes (verified Sep 2026):**
- **NTSE** (NCERT) has been suspended since the 2021-22 cycle; no revival notified. Not listed as an entry.
- **KVPY** was discontinued in 2022 and merged into **INSPIRE SHE**, but SHE itself has issued no fresh call for 2025 or 2026 (see entry). Treat the "top-1%-of-board" science scholarship route as uncertain.
- **Delhi CM Urban Leaders Fellowship** and the **Prime Minister's Rural Development Fellows** scheme are dormant/discontinued, so they're not listed.
- **Adobe India Women-in-Technology** scholarship is discontinued: no call since 2023, and [Adobe Research's programs page](https://research.adobe.com/programs/) no longer lists it. Not listed; Adobe's current India award is the PhD-level [India AI Research Fellowship](https://research.adobe.com/india-ai-research-fellowship/) (₹12L; 2026 round closed).
- Buddy4Study, WeMakeScholars etc. are aggregators. Many corporate scholarships below run their application forms *through* Buddy4Study; that is fine, but always start from the sponsor's own page linked here.

## School level (Class 9–12)

### NMMS
<!-- tags: stage=hs; field=any; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=indian-only; gates=campus-only,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** School scholarship | **Location:** India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹12,000/year, Class 9–12

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | ~Oct–Nov 2026 (state exam; each SCERT sets its own date) |
| Duration | 4 years (Class 9–12), renewal on marks |
| Who | Class 8 students in govt/govt-aided/local-body schools; ≥55% in Class 7 (50% SC/ST); parental income <₹3.5L/yr |
| Stipend | ₹12,000/yr paid through NSP |
| Stage | HS |

**What it is:** National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship. State-level MAT + SAT exam in Class 8; ~1 lakh scholarships a year distributed by state quota. Money is paid via the National Scholarship Portal.

**Reality check:** Only for government-school students. Private-school kids are out. Deadlines differ wildly by state; find your SCERT notice, not a national one.

**Apply:** [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in) (disbursal) · exam via your state SCERT

### PM YASASVI
<!-- tags: stage=hs; field=any; region=india; funding=full; citizenship=indian-only; gates=diversity,campus-only,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** School scholarship | **Location:** India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹75,000/yr (Class 9), ₹1,25,000/yr (Class 11)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | ~Oct 2026 (NSP fresh-application window; check portal) |
| Duration | Class 9–10 or Class 11–12 |
| Who | OBC / EBC / DNT students admitted to Class 9 or 11 in listed "top class" schools; parental income <₹2.5L/yr |
| Stipend | Covers fees + hostel at approved schools |
| Stage | HS |

**What it is:** Ministry of Social Justice scheme for OBC/EBC/DNT students at government-listed high-performing schools. The separate YASASVI entrance test was dropped in 2023-24; selection is now by Class 8/10 marks via NSP.

**Reality check:** The school must be on the approved list; check before assuming eligibility. Income certificate and caste certificate must be current.

**Apply:** [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)

### Central Sector Scholarship (PM-USP CSSS)
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=any; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=indian-only; gates=enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** UG/PG scholarship | **Location:** India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹12,000/yr (UG yrs 1–3), ₹20,000/yr (4th/5th yr & PG)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Oct 31, 2026** for renewals (institute verification by Nov 15). Fresh 2026-27 applications are not open: NSP lists the scheme for renewal only, so watch NSP announcements |
| Duration | Up to 5 years |
| Who | Class 12 passers in the top 20th percentile of their board; parental income <₹4.5L/yr; enrolled in regular UG/PG course; not receiving another scholarship |
| Stipend | Paid annually via DBT |
| Stage | UG / PG |

**What it is:** The default merit-cum-means scholarship for ~82,000 fresh Class 12 toppers a year. Boards send the percentile list; you just apply on NSP.

**Reality check:** Small money but nearly automatic if you meet the percentile + income gate. Institute verification is where applications die. Chase your college nodal officer.

**Apply:** [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)

### National Scholarship Portal (NSP) schemes
<!-- tags: stage=hs,ug,pg; field=any; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=indian-only; gates=diversity,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Umbrella scholarship portal | **Location:** India | **Funded:** ✅ Varies (fees + maintenance)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | ~Oct 31, 2026 usual fresh close (portal open since Jun 1, 2026); varies by scheme, with some ministries extending to Nov–Dec |
| Duration | Annual, renewable |
| Who | Post-Matric SC/ST/OBC; Minority (Pre/Post-Matric, Merit-cum-Means); PwD (Top Class Education for SwD); Top Class Education for SC; state schemes |
| Stipend | From ₹230/month maintenance to full fees at top institutes (Top Class schemes) |
| Stage | HS / UG / PG |

**What it is:** One login, ~100 central + state schemes. If you are SC/ST/OBC/minority/PwD, this is where most of your entitlement money sits, especially "Top Class Education" (full fees at IITs/IIMs/NLUs etc. for SC and PwD students).

**Reality check:** Not competitive, but document-heavy: Aadhaar-seeded bank account, income certificate, caste certificate, institute verification. Start in Aug, not Oct.

**Apply:** [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)

### PM Scholarship Scheme (KSB)
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg; field=cs,ai,ee,mech,civil,chem,med,business,law; region=india; funding=stipend; citizenship=indian-only; gates=enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** UG scholarship | **Location:** India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹2,500/month (boys), ₹3,000/month (girls)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | ~Nov 2026 (KSB usually closes fresh applications end-Nov) |
| Duration | Course duration (up to 5 yrs) |
| Who | Wards/widows of ex-servicemen, ex-Coast Guard; a parallel CAPF/Assam Rifles scheme via MHA; ≥60% in Class 12; first-year professional degree (engg, medicine, MBA, law etc.) |

> **Type:** UG science scholarship | **Location:** India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹80,000/yr (₹60k + ₹20k summer research)

|-------|---------|

**What it is:** AICTE's own PhD fellowship for AICTE-approved institutions. Since the 2024-25 revision there is no per-university quota: any eligible full-time scholar can apply, and an AICTE committee reviews the research proposal.

**Reality check:** There are two routes: register yourself on the ADF portal with your university's NOC, or come in through a university enrolled in the scheme. The fellowship counts from your coursework start only if your details are uploaded within 2 months of admission. Not for anyone who has already held ADF or NET-JRF.

**Apply:** [aicte.gov.in → ADF](https://www.aicte.gov.in/schemes/students-development-schemes/ADF-Scheme) · portal: [fellowship.aicte.gov.in](https://fellowship.aicte.gov.in)
