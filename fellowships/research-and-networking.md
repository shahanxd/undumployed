## Forums & laureate meetings

### Heidelberg Laureate Forum
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=cs,ai,math; region=europe; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** 1-week networking forum with Turing/Fields/Abel/ACM Prize laureates | **Location:** Heidelberg, Germany | **Funded:** ✅ Hotel + meals for the forum week; travel not covered by default (HLFF travel grant only if your institution won't pay)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **Feb 11, 2027** (23:59 CET) for the 14th HLF, Sep 2027; applications open Nov 11, 2026 |
| Duration | 6 days, September |
| Who | Undergraduates (≥2 years in), master's, PhD students, and postdocs (≤5 yrs post-PhD) in **mathematics or computer science**; any nationality. You apply yourself; an optional nomination from a professor/organisation strengthens the file |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** 200 young researchers chosen from ~1,000+ applicants meet ~25 laureates: lectures, workshops, boat trip, one-on-one time. Indian participants each year come from IITs, IISc, CMI, ISI, TIFR and abroad.

**Reality check:** Selection is by an international committee on academic promise plus a short motivation. Undergraduates do get in, but need real research (a paper, olympiad medal, or strong recommendation).

**Apply:** [heidelberg-laureate-forum.org → Young Researchers](https://www.heidelberg-laureate-forum.org/young-researchers/faq/)

### Lindau Nobel Laureate Meetings
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=bio,med,chem,physics,econ; region=europe; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=age-limit; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** Networking forum where ~600 young scientists meet 30–40 Nobel laureates | **Location:** Lindau, Germany | **Funded:** ✅ Registration + accommodation + meals (travel via nominating partner/DST)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | **76th Meeting (2027): Physiology/Medicine, Jun 27–Jul 2, 2027.** Academic-partner nominations **Sep 15–Oct 27, 2026**; open (self) application **Sep 8–Oct 6, 2026**. 2028 is Physics (77th meeting, Jun 25–30) alongside the 9th Economic Sciences meeting; Chemistry follows in 2029 |
| Duration | 1 week |
| Who | Undergraduates (top of class), master's, PhD students, and postdocs under 35 with no permanent position; strong research record; not previously attended |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** A week of lectures, panels and small-group sessions with 30–40 Nobel laureates. India's academic partners (DST in particular, often via an IASc/INSA/NASI joint call) nominate ~20–30 young scientists per year; you can also apply yourself through the open application.

**Reality check:** Ask your institute's international office/dean in September whether it's a Lindau academic partner. DST's "Lindau" call is usually posted on dst.gov.in in Aug–Sep. Open applications are screened harder than partner nominations.

**Apply:** [lindau-nobel.org → Young Scientists](https://www.lindau-nobel.org/young-scientists/) · [DST calls](https://dst.gov.in/call-for-proposals)

### Falling Walls Lab
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=any; region=global; funding=prize; citizenship=any; gates=; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** 3-minute research/innovation pitch competition | **Location:** ~100 local labs worldwide (India labs typically hosted with DWIH New Delhi and partner universities) → finale at Falling Walls Science Summit, Berlin | **Funded:** ✅ Local-lab winners get travel + stay for Berlin (Nov)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | 2027 season opens Feb 1, 2027; local labs run `~Mar–Sep 2027`, each with its own deadline; Berlin finale Nov 7–9, 2027 |
| Duration | 1 day (local); 3 days (Berlin) |
| Who | Bachelor's/master's/PhD students, postdocs, young professionals and entrepreneurs; any discipline; usually a few years past your last degree at most |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** Pitch "which wall does your idea break?" in 3 minutes. 100 finalists in Berlin, 3 winners, plus access to the summit (Nov 7–9 every year). Indian labs are run by the German Centre for Research and Innovation (DWIH) New Delhi and partners (e.g., IIT/IISER hosts change yearly).

**Reality check:** Entry is a short application and a 3-minute pitch; local winners go to the Berlin finale. Judges reward a concrete breakthrough over a vague "platform".

**Apply:** [falling-walls.com/lab](https://falling-walls.com/lab) · [DWIH New Delhi](https://www.dwih-newdelhi.org/en/)

## AI safety & alignment research programs

### MATS: ML Alignment & Theory Scholars
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd,grad; field=ai,cs,math,policy,bio; region=global; funding=full; citizenship=any; gates=age-limit; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** 12-week in-person research programme + optional 6–12 month funded extension | **Location:** Berkeley, CA / London | **Funded:** ✅ US$1,600/week stipend (US$19,200 for the 12 weeks) + housing + meals + US$2,000/week compute + travel + visa

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | `~Mar–Apr 2027` for Summer 2027. Winter 2027 applications closed Sep 6, 2026, for the cohort starting Jan 19, 2027. Cohorts run twice a year, Jan–Mar and Jun–Aug |
| Duration | 12 weeks full time (40 h/week), then an optional 6–12 month funded extension that over 80% of fellows get |
| Who | 18 or older by the start date, any nationality, students and professionals alike. No prior AI-safety experience needed, but you need strong technical skills (ML, maths, CS), or policy skills for the governance track, and real motivation for AI alignment and security. Seven tracks: Empirical, Theory, Strategy & Forecasting, Policy & Governance, Systems Security, Biosecurity, Founding & Field-Building |
| Stipend | US$19,200 for the 12 weeks + US$24,000 compute + housing in Berkeley (a housing stipend in London) + weekday lunches and dinners + travel + J-1 visa sponsorship for the US site |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** The main pipeline into AI-safety research: 631 alumni since 2021, 220+ papers, ~75% now working in AI alignment, transparency or security. You do mentored research with alignment, security or governance researchers from Anthropic, DeepMind, OpenAI, Redwood and academic safety labs, ending in a paper or another concrete output.

**Reality check:** Acceptance is ~5–10%. Applications run in stages and end with mentor interviews and work tests; the Empirical track expects real ML engineering, and prior ML research or strong interpretability/evals projects (e.g., from SPAR or ARENA) are the norm among admits. The Policy & Governance track is the best way in without a CS background.

**Apply:** [matsprogram.org/apply](https://www.matsprogram.org/apply)

### Anthropic Fellows Program
<!-- tags: stage=pg,phd,grad; field=ai,cs,econ,policy; region=global; funding=stipend; citizenship=any; gates=; format=hybrid -->
> **Type:** 4-month full-time research fellowship with Anthropic mentors (extension possible) | **Location:** Shared workspaces in Berkeley and London, or remote within the US, UK or Canada | **Funded:** ✅ US$3,850 / £2,310 / C$4,300 per week + ~US$15k/month compute; no visa sponsorship

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Rolling: applications are open for the cohort starting Jan 2027 and are reviewed as they arrive (the Nov 2026 cohort closed Jul 26, 2026). Several cohorts run each year, and if you can't commit to the full four months you can still apply; that is decided case by case |
| Duration | 4 months full time, with a possible extension |
| Who | Fluent Python and a strong technical background (CS, maths, physics; economics, policy or security for some workstreams); no PhD, ML experience or papers required. You must already hold (or get on your own) work authorization in the US, UK or Canada and be there for the programme, because Anthropic does not sponsor fellows' visas. From India, that means it only works if you already study or work in one of those countries (for example on OPT after a US degree, or on a UK Graduate visa) |
| Stipend | US$3,850 / £2,310 / C$4,300 a week + ~US$15k/month compute + mentorship from Anthropic researchers |
| Stage | PG / PhD / professionals |

**What it is:** Four months of full-time, mentored research with Anthropic, aiming at a public output such as a paper. There are three workstreams, each with its own posting: AI safety & security (scalable oversight, adversarial robustness, mechanistic interpretability, model organisms, AI welfare, vulnerability research, red teaming), ML systems & reinforcement learning, and economics & policy with the Anthropic Institute. In past cohorts, 25–50% of fellows received a full-time offer.

**Reality check:** Very selective; MATS, SPAR and LASR alumni are the typical intake. Anthropic doesn't run a student internship, so this is the way in; ignore listings for an "Anthropic internship India".
