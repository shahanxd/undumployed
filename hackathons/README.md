## India: national & community

### Smart India Hackathon (SIH)
<!-- tags: stage=hs,ug,pg; field=cs,ai,ee,mech; region=india; funding=prize; citizenship=indian-only; gates=nomination,campus-only,team,enrolled-only; format=in-person -->
> **Type:** National hackathon (Ministry of Education / AICTE / MIC) | **Location:** Nodal centres across India | **Funded:** ✅ ₹1,00,000 per winning team per problem statement (typical); travel/stay at finale covered by nodal centre

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | SIH 2026 is open: problem statements are up and SPOC login is live on sih.gov.in. Expected: internal hackathons `~Sep–Oct 2026`, idea submission by your SPOC `~Oct 2026`, grand finale `~Dec 2026`. Your SPOC has the exact dates, so ask now. |
| Duration | Internal round (1 day) → 36-hour grand finale |
| Who | Teams of 6 (at least one woman) from AICTE/UGC-recognised institutions, nominated by your college's SPOC; **you cannot enter directly**. Also a **SIH Junior** school track (classes 6–12) via school SPOC. |
| Prize | ₹1 lakh per PS (software/hardware); student-innovation PS prizes tiered |
| Stage | HS (Junior) / UG / PG |

**What it is:** Government and industry problem statements; hundreds of teams at the finale across nodal centres. Winners frequently get follow-on work from the ministry/PSU that set the problem.

**Reality check:** Your college's internal hackathon is the real filter and is often chaotic, so know your SPOC by August. Hardware PS have far fewer teams per slot than software.

**Apply:** [sih.gov.in](https://sih.gov.in) (via institution SPOC)

## India: corporate hackathons

Most of these are hiring funnels disguised as hackathons: winners get pre-placement interviews (PPIs). Several run mid-year on **Unstop**, and Flipkart GRiD and Myntra HackerRamp now run on **MyCareernet**. Dates drift year to year, so treat all as `~`.

### Flipkart GRiD
<!-- tags: stage=ug,pg,phd; field=cs,ai; region=india; funding=prize; citizenship=indian-only; gates=enrolled-only; format=hybrid -->
> **Type:** National engineering challenge | **Location:** Online → finale at Flipkart HQ, Bengaluru | **Funded:** ✅ Prizes + PPIs/PPOs; finalists' travel and stay covered

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | GRiD 8.0 took registrations **Jun 19–Jul 12, 2026** (closed). Next: `~Jun–Jul 2027` |
| Duration | Three online rounds, then a live finale at Flipkart HQ, Bengaluru |
| Who | 2026 rules: students at any engineering college in India (B.Tech/B.E., M.Tech/M.E., M.S., PhD, or a dual/integrated degree) graduating 2027–2030, or up to 2031 for PhD and dual/integrated degrees. You register on your own; finalists are put into teams of four. Tracks: Software Development, AI Engineering and Data Science |
| Stage | UG / PG / PhD |

**What it is:** Flipkart's flagship off-campus hiring challenge. Round 1 screens your profile (academics and achievements), Round 2 is a 90-minute proctored coding test, Round 3 asks you to code a solution to a Flipkart business case, and the top 48 (16 per track) build and present a prototype at the live finale. Flipkart arranges finalists' travel and stay. Winners and high scorers get interviews for summer internships (3rd-years), and for winter internships and full-time roles (4th-years).

**Reality check:** The coding test is the real cut: tens of thousands register and only a few hundred reach the case-study round. The interview, not the prize, is what you're playing for. For Flipkart's direct internships, see [tech-companies.md](../internships/tech-companies.md#flipkart-grid--direct-internships).

**Apply:** [GRiD 8.0 on MyCareernet](https://mycareernet.co/events/flipkart-earlycareers-grid-8-0/) (Flipkart's official registration page, run by HirePro; GRiD left Unstop in 2025)

## Hackathon travel reimbursement: who flies Indian students

| Hackathon | Policy (Sep 2026) | Realistic coverage from India |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| **TreeHacks** | Flights with regional caps, food, swag | Partial (cap < India→SFO fare); still the best deal |
| **HackMIT** | Travel reimbursement up to a regional cap for every attendee; must submit a project | Partial; historically some full international reimbursements |
| **Hack the North** | Partial travel reimbursement for accepted hackers (flight cap on the RSVP dashboard; paid only if you attend, submit and are judged) + free shuttle from Toronto Pearson | Partial; Canadian visa lead time is the real barrier |
| **Cal Hacks** | Limited travel stipends, regional caps, requested in application | Partial/unlikely for international |
| **PennApps** | Limited reimbursement, not guaranteed | Unlikely |
| **JPMorgan Code for Good (India)** | Travel + stay covered for selected students | Full (domestic) |
| **SIH grand finale** | Travel/stay handled by nodal centre/institution | Full (domestic) |
| **Junction** | Limited travel grants for the strongest applications; separate deadline (Sep 6 in 2026) | Rare |
| **HackHarvard, nwHacks, UofTHacks, Hack the 6ix, LA Hacks, ETHGlobal, MIT Reality Hack** | No travel support | Self-funded |

Rule of thumb: apply to TreeHacks, HackMIT and Hack the North every year, and get a 10-year US B-1/B-2 early. A first US interview takes 7–12 months to get and a Canadian visitor visa 1–4+ months (see the [visa guide](../guides/visas-for-indian-passport.md)); without one in hand, the US and Canadian events here are out of reach.
