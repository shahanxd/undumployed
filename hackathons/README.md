### Google Summer of Code (GSoC)
> **Type:** Open source internship | **Remote** | **Funded:** ✅ ₹60,000–2,50,000 (India, PPP-adjusted by project size)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Contributor applications: ~March annually. Org list announced Jan–Feb. |
| Duration | 3 months |
| Who | Students 18+. No geographic restriction. |

**What it is:** 3-month paid open source development program. You work with a real open source organization on a project you propose. One of the best resume items available.

**Reality check:** You must contribute to the organization's codebase months before applications open. Community engagement is tracked. Showing up at application time with zero prior contributions = very low chance.

**Prep steps:**
1. Start exploring GSoC orgs now at summerofcode.withgoogle.com
2. Pick 1–2 orgs whose codebase interests you
3. Make meaningful contributions (bug fixes, features) and engage in their community
4. Proposals that reference your prior contributions get priority

**Apply:** [summerofcode.withgoogle.com](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com) — watch for 2027 cycle (opens Jan–Feb)

### Smart India Hackathon (SIH)
> **Type:** National Hackathon | **Location:** India (distributed) | **Funded:** ✅ Prize money + Grand Finale

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | Internal institution hackathons: September 2026. Grand Finale: December 2026. |
| Who | Engineering students across India. **Must participate through your institution.** |

**What it is:** India's largest national hackathon — organized by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell + AICTE. 36-hour Grand Finale in December. Real problem statements from government and industry.

**How it works:** Institutions conduct internal hackathons in September → selected teams go to Grand Finale in December. You cannot enter directly — go through your college's SPOC/HOD.

**Apply:** [sih.gov.in](https://sih.gov.in) + check with your college SPOC

### Outreachy
> **Type:** Open source internship | **Remote** | **Funded:** ✅ $7,000 for 3 months

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Deadline | May cohort: Jan–Feb applications. December cohort: Aug–Sep applications. |
| Duration | 3 months |
| Who | People subject to systemic bias or underrepresentation in tech in their country. |

**What it is:** Paid remote internship working on open source and free software. Legitimate and real — organizations include GNOME, Linux kernel projects, Mozilla, and more. Includes a contribution period before final selection.

**Reality check:** Lower profile than GSoC but real stipend and real work. Good for those who don't qualify for GSoC yet or want an alternative track.

**Apply:** [outreachy.org](https://www.outreachy.org)
