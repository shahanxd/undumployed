# Application Kit

> Build this once. Update it every 3 months. It unlocks most opportunities.

---

## The Standard Kit

| Document | Used For | Status |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Resume / CV** | Everything | Build first |
| **Statement of Purpose (SoP)** | Research internships, fellowships, grad programs | Tailor per application |
| **Transcripts** | Almost all international programs | Get official + scan unofficial |
| **Letter of Recommendation (LOR)** | Research programs, fellowships | Ask 3+ months in advance |
| **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** | DRDO, ISRO, some IIT programs | From college admin |
| **GitHub profile** | All tech roles, GSoC, open source | Clean + pinned projects |
| **Portfolio / Media Kit** | Insider programs, design roles | Optional but high-impact |

---

## Resume

**For research programs:**
- Use a 2-column academic CV format
- List research experience, publications, projects, courses, conferences
- Include CGPA, relevant coursework
- Europass format for European programs (ETH, EPFL, DAAD)

**For corporate roles:**
- 1 page maximum
- Reverse chronological — most recent first
- Quantify everything: "Built X that reduced Y by Z%"
- No photo, no address beyond city

**Tools:** LaTeX (Overleaf), Canva, or a clean Word template. Avoid decorative templates.

## Statement of Purpose (SoP)

**Structure that works:**
1. **Opening** — the specific problem or question you're interested in (not "I have always been passionate about...")
2. **Your background** — what you've done that's relevant. Specific, not generic.
3. **Why this program** — reference specific faculty, projects, labs, or features. Show you researched.
4. **What you'll contribute** — concrete, not vague aspirations.
5. **What's next** — how this fits your longer-term direction.

**Length:** 1–2 pages. Some programs specify word limits — follow them exactly.

**Common mistakes:**
- Generic openers about childhood curiosity
- No specific reference to the program's faculty or research
- Listing what the program offers without saying what you bring
- Passive voice throughout

## Letter of Recommendation (LOR)

**Ask early — 2–3 months minimum before deadline.**

**Who to ask:**
- A professor who has seen you work on a project or research
- A supervisor from an internship or research position
- Not a professor whose class you attended without other interaction

**What to give them:**
- Your CV / resume
- The program you're applying to and its deadline
- What you want them to highlight (specific project, skill, quality)
- A brief reminder of the work you did together

**Waive your right to view it** — letters are more credible when waived.

## Transcripts

- Get an official sealed transcript from your university
- Keep 5–10 photocopies of unofficial transcripts scanned as PDF
- For programs requiring conversion to 4.0 scale: use WES (World Education Services) if required, or calculate using the program's stated conversion method

## NOC (No Objection Certificate)

Required for: DRDO, ISRO, some IIT programs, government labs.

**How to get one:**
1. Email your college's Academic/Dean office with the internship details
2. Specify: organization name, dates, nature of work
3. Get the letter on college letterhead with a seal

Allow 1–2 weeks processing time.

## GitHub Profile

Critical for: GSoC, LFX, Outreachy, all tech roles, research programs with a CS component.

**What makes a strong GitHub:**
- **Pinned repositories** — pin your 4–6 best projects
- **README files** — each pinned project should have a clear README explaining what it does, why you built it, and how to run it
- **Commit history** — consistent contributions, not one giant commit
- **Contributions to open source** — even small bug fixes or documentation
- **Green squares** — activity graph matters less than quality, but showing consistent work helps

**Before applying:** Go through your profile as a stranger. Is it clear what you build?

---

## For Insider Programs: Media Kit

A simple 1-page PDF or link with:
- Your name and city
- Your platforms (Instagram/X/YouTube/blog) + follower counts
- Content samples (3–5 best posts or pieces)
- Contact email

Not required but high-impact for brand photowalks and insider applications.
