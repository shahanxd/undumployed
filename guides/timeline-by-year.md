**Reading the calendar:** Aug–Oct is corporate + fellowship season. Oct–Mar is international research season. Nov–Apr is Indian research season (the IAS SRFP closes in late November, most IIT portals run Feb–Apr), and GSoC is in March. Summer is for *doing*, not applying. Still, keep an eye on July openings.

## Class 9–10

You are early. The goal is not "opportunities" but one real skill and one real thing you made.

**Programmes that fit**
- Olympiad pipeline: IOQM → RMO → INMO (math); NSEJS (junior science, classes 8–10); ZIO/ZCO → INOI (informatics; any school student up to class 12, with no minimum age). See [Competitive programming](../competitions/competitive-programming.md) for informatics and [Case, business & research competitions](../competitions/case-business-and-research.md) for the maths and science olympiads.
- Breakthrough Junior Challenge (13–18; 2-minute science video; opens ~May, closes ~mid-Sep), listed in [Case, business & research competitions](../competitions/case-business-and-research.md).
- Beginner open source: Hacktoberfest (October; anyone can join with a free MyMLH account, and pull requests no longer count toward rewards) and GirlScript Summer of Code (~Feb; school students welcome). More in [Open source](../open-source/README.md).
- Junior hackathons on Devfolio/MLH that allow under-18s with guardian consent (see [Hackathons](../hackathons/README.md)).
- Free tools: GitHub Student Developer Pack (13+; needs school email or ID). See [Student perks](../student-perks/README.md).
- Local: IIT/IISER open days, NIAS/Nehru Planetarium talks, GDG DevFest in your city (Oct–Dec; free or ₹200–500). See [Events: India](../events/india.md).

**Calendar:** Jun–Jul IOQM registration (exam ~Sep) · Oct ICO registration, then ZCO/ZIO ~late Nov–Dec · Nov RMO/NSEJS · Jan–Feb INMO/INOI · May–Sep Breakthrough Junior · Oct Hacktoberfest.

**Build this year:** one language properly (Python), one project with a README on GitHub, an olympiad problem-solving habit (AoPS / CSES beginner set), and a habit of reading (Quanta, Bartosz Ciechanowski, 3Blue1Brown).

**Mistakes:** paying for "certificate" internships or hackathon "participation certificates"; chasing 10 things instead of one olympiad; assuming you need to be in an IIT-coaching city to do INOI (ZCO, the first round, is online, and INOI has centres across India).

## Class 11–12

Two years where a few competitions change your college options, and the year before college, when you should apply to things most people don't know exist.

**Programmes that fit**
- Olympiads: IOQM/RMO/INMO (→ IMO), NSEP/NSEC/NSEB/NSEA → INO (→ IPhO/IChO/IBO/IOAA), ZIO/ZCO → INOI (→ IOI). See [Competitive programming](../competitions/competitive-programming.md) and the maths and science olympiad entries in [Case, business & research competitions](../competitions/case-business-and-research.md).
- Research at 17: IRIS National Fair → Regeneron ISEF; Breakthrough Junior; Conrad Challenge; Diamond Challenge; John Locke Essay; Wharton Global HS Investment Competition; Yale Young Global Scholars (you pay unless you get aid). See [Case, business & research competitions](../competitions/case-business-and-research.md).
- Scholarships that need class-12 marks: INSPIRE-SHE (which replaced KVPY; top 1% in boards or a top JEE Advanced/NEET rank, for a natural-science degree), though it has issued no call since the 2024 batch, so don't count on it; state merit scholarships via NSP (closes ~Oct 31). Details in [Fellowships: India](../fellowships/india.md).
- Study abroad at UG level: full-aid US colleges (need-blind for internationals: MIT, Harvard, Yale, Princeton, Amherst, Dartmouth…), Rhodes-track thinking starts now; UK via UCAS; Ashoka/Plaksha/Krea scholarships. More in [Study abroad](../fellowships/study-abroad.md).
- Hackathons (age 16+ with consent on most Devfolio/MLH events); Smart India Hackathon has a **junior** track for classes 6–12, entered through your school (see [Hackathons](../hackathons/README.md)).
- Communities: Google Developer Groups on Campus (formerly GDSC) are for college students, but GDG city chapters (DevFest, local meetups) are open to anyone, and Hack Club (ages 13–18) is built for school students. See [Ambassador programmes](../communities/ambassador-programs.md).
- Perks: GitHub Student Pack, Figma, Canva for Education (through your school; it doesn't cover college students). Details in [Student perks](../student-perks/README.md).

**Calendar**
| Months | Do |
|---|---|
| Aug–Sep | IOQM (~early Sep); NSE olympiad enrolment (~Aug–Sep); Wharton HS investment comp (register by ~mid-Sep); IRIS fair registration (Aug 1 to ~early Oct); US Common App essays (if applying abroad) |
| Oct–Nov | ICO registration (Oct), then ZCO/ZIO (~late Nov–Dec); RMO (~mid-Nov); NSE olympiads (~late Nov); Conrad Challenge (~late Oct, then ~early Jan); US early decision (Nov 1) |
| Dec–Jan | INMO (~mid-Jan), INO (~late Jan), INOI (~Jan–Feb); US regular decision (Jan 1–5); UK UCAS (late Jan); Diamond Challenge (~mid-Jan) |
| Feb–Mar | JEE/NEET/CUET; John Locke registration (by ~Mar 31); SAT if needed |
| Apr–Jun | Board results; INSPIRE-SHE (only if a call appears); John Locke essay (due ~May 31); Breakthrough Junior opens (~May); IOQM registration opens (~Jun); college decisions; first hackathon |

**Build this year:** one olympiad stage cleared (even RMO/ZCO is a resume line at 17), one 2-minute video or essay you're proud of, SAT/IELTS *only* if applying abroad, a GitHub with 2 projects.

**Mistakes:** treating class 12 boards as the only thing (they matter less than you think outside NIRF-ranked Indian admissions); missing IOQM registration because it closes in July, weeks before the September exam; applying to US colleges without checking need-blind/need-aware policies for internationals.

## 1st year undergrad

Almost nothing formal is open to you, and that is fine. This is the year to become the person 3rd-year opportunities are designed for.

**Programmes that fit**
- Big Tech's first-internship programmes start in 2nd year: Microsoft Explore (~Aug–Oct) and Google STEP India (~Nov–Dec; aimed at women and students with disabilities) take only 2nd-years, so note the dates for next year. See [Tech companies](../internships/tech-companies.md).
- Hackathons, as many as you can stand: SIH (team from your college), Devfolio/MLH weekend events, ETHGlobal (18+; Mumbai in Nov 2026), and HackMIT/TreeHacks if you already hold a US visa (both are in person, with capped travel reimbursement). More in [Hackathons](../hackathons/README.md).
- Open source entry: Hacktoberfest (Oct), Kharagpur Winter of Code (Dec), GirlScript SoC (~Feb); Google Summer of Code is open from 18+ (you *can* apply in 1st year; most winners have prior contributions). See [Open source](../open-source/README.md).
- Competitive programming: Codeforces/AtCoder weekly; ICPC (teams register by ~late Sep and sit an online prelim in early Oct; 1st years do compete). Details in [Competitive programming](../competitions/competitive-programming.md).
- Research in India that takes 1st-years: IIT Gandhinagar SRIP (any bachelor's student, all disciplines; ~Feb–Mar), JNCASR's Summer Research Fellowship (1st–2nd-year BSc in life sciences; ~Jan 31), IISER Pune and IISER Bhopal summer programmes (unpaid). The IAS SRFP opens to you in 2nd year. See [Research India](../internships/research-india.md).
- Student communities: GDG on Campus, Microsoft Student Ambassadors (rolling, no application), AWS Cloud Clubs (now AWS Student Builder Groups; 18+, captain apps ~early Oct), Notion Campus Leaders (~Apr–May). More in [Ambassador programmes](../communities/ambassador-programs.md).
- Perks: GitHub Student Pack, Azure for Students, JetBrains, Figma, and Google AI Pro free for Indian college students 18+ (redeem by Dec 31, 2026). See [Student perks](../student-perks/README.md).
- Case comps with no year limit: Tata Imagination Challenge (~Aug–Sep), Tata Crucible Campus Quiz (~Feb–Mar), Hult Prize campus round (Sep–Feb; every teammate 18+). See [Case, business & research](../competitions/case-business-and-research.md).

**Calendar**
| Months | Do |
|---|---|
| Aug–Sep | join CP club · ICPC team (register by ~late Sep) · SIH team (college rounds ~Sep–Oct) · note the Explore and STEP dates for next year |
| Oct–Dec | Hacktoberfest · Meta Hacker Cup (~Oct–Dec) · ICPC prelim and regionals as a rookie · KWoC (sign-ups ~late Nov) · NASA Space Apps (~mid-Nov) · ETHGlobal Mumbai (Nov) |
| Jan–Mar | GSoC org scouting → contributions → **apply in Mar** · JNCASR SRFP (~Jan 31, if your field takes 1st-years) · IIT Gandhinagar SRIP / IISER (Feb–Mar) · Google Solution Challenge (~Mar–Apr) |
| Apr–Jul | Summer: GSoC or a self-built project or a startup internship (see [Startups](../startups/README.md)); Google ambassador apps (Gemini Student Ambassador ~Apr, GDG on Campus ~Apr–Jun); Flipkart GRiD (~Jun–Jul) |

**Build this year:** DSA foundation (one sheet: Striver A2Z or NeetCode 150, done properly), 2–3 shipped projects, 30+ Codeforces contests, one open-source PR merged, a LinkedIn that isn't embarrassing.

**Mistakes:** spending the year on "web dev tutorial hell" instead of one deep project; ignoring CP because "I'm not a competitive person" (it is the gate for every Big Tech OA); not joining the college's coding/robotics/consulting club in the first month.

## 2nd year undergrad

The first year where doors actually open, and where the *summer* decides how your 3rd-year applications look.

**Programmes that fit**
- Microsoft Explore (~Aug–Oct) and Google STEP India (~Nov–Dec; aimed at women and students with disabilities): 2nd year is the only year you can apply. Amazon Future Engineer scholars can interview for an 8-week Amazon internship after 2nd year. Startup internships via [Startups](../startups/README.md) and [Tech companies](../internships/tech-companies.md).
- IIT summer research: IIT Gandhinagar SRIP, IIT Kanpur SURGE, IIT Madras SFP, IIT Roorkee SPARK, IIT Kharagpur GRISHMA, IISc, IISER, IIIT Hyderabad (faculty-first; some centres take strong 2nd-years), IAS SRFP (BSc 2nd year, BTech 2nd–3rd year; closes Nov 30). TIFR Hyderabad's VSRP and IUCAA start from 3rd year. Details in [Research India](../internships/research-india.md).
- International research that takes 2nd years: Summer@EPFL (2nd year+, CS/EE; Nov 29), ETH SSRF (2 years done by the start; ~Dec 16), UTRIP (natural sciences; ~Jan 7), Amgen Scholars Asia (biosciences; Feb 1), IRIS@NUS (closes ~late Aug for the following summer) and NUS SoC summer research (~mid-Jan). Mitacs Globalink (B.E./B.Tech only; you need 1–2 semesters *left after* the internship, so you apply at the start of **3rd year**, Jul–Sep), DAAD WISE and A*STAR SIPGA are 3rd-year programmes. See [Research global](../internships/research-global.md).
- GSoC (Mar), LFX Mentorship (3 terms), Outreachy (for groups underrepresented in tech; apply ~early Feb for the May cohort, which suits Indian semesters; the December cohort closes ~end Aug), MLH Fellowship. More in [Open source](../open-source/README.md).
- ICPC (register by ~late Sep; regionals Dec–Jan), Meta Hacker Cup (~Oct–Dec), Flipkart GRiD (~Jun–Jul), TCS CodeVita (Round 1 ~Nov–Dec). See [Competitive programming](../competitions/competitive-programming.md).
- Case/quant comps: Jane Street SEE India (1st/2nd-years; closes ~late Sep), Optiver/IMC student events, Citadel Datathon (18+), Tata Crucible (~Feb–Mar). See [Case, business & research](../competitions/case-business-and-research.md) and [Finance, quant & consulting](../internships/finance-quant-consulting.md).
- Government/non-tech: NITI Aayog internship (applications open the 1st–10th of every month, once you've finished 2 years and with 85%+ in Class 12), ministry internships via the AICTE portal. The MEA internship needs a degree, so it comes later. See [Government & non-tech](../internships/government-and-non-tech.md).
- Communities/ambassador: GDG on Campus lead (~Apr–Jun), GitHub Campus Experts (~Jul; you need a track record of running a community first), Microsoft Student Ambassadors (rolling). More in [Ambassador programmes](../communities/ambassador-programs.md).

**Calendar**
| Months | Do |
|---|---|
| Aug–Sep | Microsoft Explore (~Aug–Oct) · Jane Street SEE (closes ~late Sep) · SIH · ICPC team (register by ~late Sep) · **check that your college is on Mitacs' India list** (you apply at the start of 3rd year) |
| Oct–Nov | Hacktoberfest · Meta Hacker Cup · NASA Space Apps (~mid-Nov) · Google STEP India (~Nov–mid-Dec) · Summer@EPFL (Nov 29) · IAS SRFP (Nov 30) |
| Dec–Jan | ETH SSRF (~Dec 16) · UTokyo UTRIP (~Jan 7) · NUS SoC summer research (~mid-Jan) · JNCASR SRFP (~Jan 31) · CERN (needs 6 semesters done, so not until 3rd year) · KWoC |
| Feb–Mar | **IIT summer programmes (SURGE, SFP, SRIP and SPARK close Feb–early Mar; GRISHMA follows in April), IISER Pune** · Charpak Summer Training · **GSoC (Mar)** · Amgen Scholars Asia (Feb 1) · Outreachy (~early Feb) |
| Apr–Jul | Summer internship/research · IIT Kharagpur GRISHMA (~Apr 5–18) · C4GT · GDG lead apps · Flipkart GRiD (~Jun–Jul) · Mitacs opens (~Jul; this is your application, for the summer after 3rd year) · Rhodes thinking starts (you apply the summer before final year) |

**Build this year:** one research-flavoured summer (IIT/IISc/IAS SRFP/GSoC), a project with users or a paper-in-progress, DSA at "can solve LeetCode medium in 25 min", one recommendation-quality relationship with a professor, a 1-page resume in Jake's template ([Application Kit](application-kit.md)).

**Mistakes:** applying to 30 IIT programmes with the same SoP (they read them; see the [cold-email guide](how-to-cold-email.md)); missing Mitacs at the start of 3rd year because you never checked whether your college is on its India list (it takes B.E./B.Tech students only); treating summer as "rest" (the 3rd-year Big Tech resume screen looks at *this* summer).

## 3rd year / pre-final

The application year that matters most. Big Tech, quant, consulting and every major international research programme target the *penultimate* year.

**Programmes that fit**
- **Big Tech SWE internships (India):** Google SWE, Microsoft SWE, Amazon SDE, Adobe, Nvidia, Qualcomm, Intel, Samsung R&D, Uber, Atlassian, Apple, Oracle, Salesforce, Cisco, Texas Instruments. See [Tech companies](../internships/tech-companies.md).
- **Quant/finance/consulting:** Jane Street, DE Shaw, Optiver, IMC, Tower, Quadeye, Graviton, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, McKinsey, BCG/BCG X, Bain, Kearney. Details in [Finance, quant & consulting](../internships/finance-quant-consulting.md).
- **International research (summer between 3rd and 4th):** CERN Summer Student, ETH SSRF, Summer@EPFL, Caltech SURF (find a Caltech mentor first), Mitacs (B.E./B.Tech at colleges on its India list; closes ~mid-Sep at the start of 3rd year), DAAD WISE (if DAAD reopens it), Max Planck, RIKEN (your university has to request the placement), UTRIP, HKUST IVISP, KAIST, NTU/NUS, A*STAR SIPGA, Amgen Scholars Asia, Charpak Summer Training. OIST (Oct 15 or ~Apr 15) runs 4–6 months, so it needs a semester off. See [Research global](../internships/research-global.md).
- **India research:** IIT/IISc/IISER as above, TIFR Hyderabad VSRP, IUCAA, PRL, RRI, NCBS/InStem, CSIR labs, DRDO (rolling; NOC; summer slots fill by ~Feb), IMSc Chennai and HRI Prayagraj for a DAE-institute summer (BARC's OCES is a job programme, not an internship), ISRO (its guidelines want final-year UG, so it's usually a final-year project; NOC). Details in [Research India](../internships/research-india.md).
- **Open source:** GSoC (3rd-year applicants dominate), LFX, MLH Fellowship, Summer of Bitcoin. More in [Open source](../open-source/README.md).
- **Fellowships to *start* preparing:** Rhodes (you apply ~Jun–Jul, the summer before final year), Schwarzman (final year or after), Fulbright, Commonwealth, DAAD, Erasmus Mundus (for master's right after). See [Study abroad](../fellowships/study-abroad.md) and [Research & networking](../fellowships/research-and-networking.md).
- **Government:** NITI Aayog, MeitY's Digital India internship (~Apr; for students in the second-last year of their course), RBI (PG and law students), MEA and LAMP (after graduation), ministry internships. See [Government & non-tech](../internships/government-and-non-tech.md).
- **Competitions:** ICPC (last serious shot), Citadel Datathon, Flipkart GRiD, Hult Prize. See [Competitive programming](../competitions/competitive-programming.md) and [Case, business & research](../competitions/case-business-and-research.md).
- **Startups:** EF India (final year+), YC (co-founder in final year is fine; Early Decision lets students apply now and join after graduating), Peak XV Surge (later), Polaris Fellowship (~May–Jun). More in [Startups](../startups/README.md).

| Months | Do |
|---|---|
| **Aug–Oct** | **Big Tech intern applications** (on-campus if you have it; off-campus careers pages + referrals otherwise; Google SWE India ~Sep–Nov) · quant (Jane Street/DE Shaw/Optiver) · consulting summer internships (McK/BCG/Bain via campus, Sep–Dec) · Goldman/JPM/MS (apply Jul–Sep; Goldman opens ~Jul 1) · **Mitacs (closes ~mid-Sep)** · ICPC team registration (closes ~late Sep) |
| **Oct–Jan** | **International research deadlines**: Summer@EPFL (Nov 29) → IAS SRFP (Nov 30) → ETH SSRF (~Dec 16) → UTRIP (~Jan 7) → CERN (~late Jan) → Amgen Scholars Asia (Feb 1) → Caltech SURF (Mar 1) · DAAD WISE only if DAAD reopens it (past calls closed ~Nov 1) · cold-email PIs Oct–Dec for RIKEN/MPI/HKUST/Caltech ([cold-email guide](how-to-cold-email.md)) · Kharagpur WoC |
| **Jan–Mar** | **IIT summer programmes** (SURGE, SFP, SRIP and SPARK close Feb–early Mar; GRISHMA runs in April) · TIFR Hyderabad VSRP and JNCASR SRFP (~Jan 31) · IUCAA (Mar–early Apr) · Charpak Summer Training (~Feb–Mar) · **GSoC (Mar)** · MLH Fellowship · start GRE/TOEFL if going abroad for master's |
| **Apr–Jul** | Summer internship · convert PPO by delivering · IIT Kharagpur GRISHMA (~Apr 5–18) · **Rhodes India (~Jun 1–Jul 23)**: this is your round if you graduate next year, so line up 5–8 referees in spring |

**Build this year:** a summer internship that converts (PPO) *or* a research summer that produces a paper/poster; LeetCode ~300 with all patterns; system-design basics; 2 strong referees; a draft SoP; a GitHub that a recruiter understands in 30 seconds.

**Mistakes:** leaving Big Tech applications to November (campus drives run Aug–Oct, and off-campus postings close within weeks); sending one generic SoP to ETH/EPFL/CERN (they are very different programmes); not asking for referee letters until December (professors need 4+ weeks; see [Application Kit](application-kit.md)).

## Final year

Two tracks run in parallel: **jobs/PPO** and **what next** (master's, PhD, fellowship, startup). Most people should pick one primary.

**Programmes that fit**
- **Placements / new-grad:** Big Tech new-grad (Aug–Oct), quant new-grad, consulting BA/associate (Aug–Sep), off-campus via careers pages (rolling), TCS NQT/Infosys/Wipro/Accenture mass hiring (Aug–Feb). See [Tech companies](../internships/tech-companies.md), [Finance, quant & consulting](../internships/finance-quant-consulting.md) and [Interviews & tests](interviews-and-tests.md).

Your opportunities are conferences, visiting positions, fellowships that top up your stipend, and industry research internships. Your calendar is your field's conference cycle.

**Programmes that fit**

Details in [Research & networking](../fellowships/research-and-networking.md) and [Study abroad](../fellowships/study-abroad.md).
- Networking/prestige: Heidelberg Laureate Forum (Feb), Lindau Nobel Meetings (self-apply by ~early Oct, or through a partner nomination by ~late Oct; the subject rotates each year), Global Young Scientists Summit Singapore (nomination), Rising Stars workshops (EECS, ML), Cornell/MIT/Berkeley visiting days. See [Research & networking](../fellowships/research-and-networking.md).

**Calendar:** Aug–Nov industry research intern apps (US summer) · Sep–Oct Lindau (self-apply by ~early Oct, partner nominations by ~late Oct) · Oct–Nov DAAD research grants · Oct–Dec Raman-Charpak · Feb HLF · Mar–May fellowship nominations and calls (Google via your university ~Mar–Apr, Qualcomm India ~Mar–May) · ~Jul 1 Fulbright-Nehru Doctoral · Rolling: travel grants (usually 8–12 weeks before the conference) · Your field's paper deadlines dictate the rest.

**Build this year:** visibility. That means a talk outside your institute, a Twitter/X or Bluesky or blog presence that explains your work, one collaboration outside your lab, and a CV that lists grants you *won*, not just papers.

**Mistakes:** never leaving the lab (a visiting stint is the best PhD-year decision most people don't make); missing nomination-only programmes because you didn't ask your department (GYSS and the Google PhD Fellowship need institutional nomination, and Lindau's partner route is an easier door than its open application); applying to industry research internships in January (they're gone).

## Took a gap / graduated & unemployed

This repo is called *undumployed* for a reason. Honest version: most "student" programmes exclude you, some of the best ones don't, and the ones that don't are usually better than the campus route anyway.

**Build this year (the honest list):**
1. A single public artefact per month: a merged PR, a blog post, a Kaggle notebook, a shipped feature. Twelve months of that beats any certificate.

**Mistakes:** paying for a "job guarantee" bootcamp when GSoC/Outreachy/LFX are free and better-regarded; hiding the gap (interviewers handle honesty well and evasiveness badly); assuming you need a college ID for everything. Most things on this page don't ask for one.

## If you're not from an IIT/NIT

True things: on-campus Big Tech, quant and consulting recruiting is concentrated in ~30 campuses; most *research* programmes and *all* open-source programmes are campus-blind; international research programmes look at your CGPA, professor's letter and what you did, not your college's NIRF rank.

**What actually works off-campus**
- **Open source is the great equaliser.** GSoC/LFX/Outreachy selections are based on contributions made *before* applying. A GSoC on the resume gets you Big Tech OA links from recruiters. [Open source](../open-source/README.md)
- **Referrals over portals.** Every Big Tech India office has an internal referral system; a warm intro from a GSoC mentor, a hackathon judge, an alumnus or a LinkedIn message with a specific project link gets you into the OA. Apply on the careers page *and* get referred.
- **Competitive programming ratings are read.** Codeforces ≥1600 / Expert or ICPC regionalist is a filter that overrides college on many quant and product-company screens. [Competitive programming](../competitions/competitive-programming.md)
- **Research: go to the professor, not the portal.** IIT/IISc summer programmes accept non-IIT students, and the *faculty-first* route (cold email + professor confirmation) has a higher hit rate than the central portal. [cold-email guide](how-to-cold-email.md), [Research India](../internships/research-india.md)
- **International programmes that are campus-blind by design:** Mitacs (B.E./B.Tech only, at colleges on its India list; IITs were left off for 2027, so check yours is on it), DAAD WISE (any AICTE/UGC-recognised institution, if DAAD reopens it), Summer@EPFL, ETH SSRF, CERN, OIST, Charpak. See [Research global](../internships/research-global.md).
- **Hackathon wins convert.** SIH winners, ETHGlobal/Devfolio finalists and Flipkart GRiD finalists get direct recruiter reach-outs. [Hackathons](../hackathons/README.md)
- **Off-campus mass drives** (TCS NQT, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Capgemini) are the safety net. Infosys HackWithInfy / TCS CodeVita ranks lead to *better* roles (Power Programmer / Digital) than the campus pool. [Interviews & tests](interviews-and-tests.md)
- **Startups hire on GitHub, not college.** [Startups](../startups/README.md)

**What to stop doing:** waiting for a placement cell that doesn't bring the companies you want; collecting Coursera certificates; applying to 200 jobs via portals with the same resume.
