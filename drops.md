# Drops

One-off and limited-edition opportunities: a hackathon a lab runs once, a sponsored sprint, a first cohort. They're announced a few weeks ahead and gone a month later, so they get their own list. Each one is a normal entry in its category file (a hackathon drop is also under [Hackathons](hackathons/README.md)), and this page is **generated** from them by `scripts/build_drops.py`.
